How BIG is Jannah? 🌿✨

Every fact in this reel is from the Quran and authentic hadith:

• A space in Jannah the size of a whip is better than the whole world (Sahih al-Bukhari 3250)
• A tent made of one hollow pearl, 60 miles wide (Sahih al-Bukhari 4879)
• A tree whose shade a rider can't cross in 100 years (Sahih al-Bukhari 3251)
• The space between the two sides of ONE gate: like Makkah to Busra (Sahih Muslim 194)
• 100 levels, each as far apart as the sky and the earth (Sahih al-Bukhari 2790)
• Its width is like the heavens and the earth (Surah Al-Imran 3:133)
• The LAST person to enter Jannah gets this world… and 10 times more (Sahih al-Bukhari 6571)
• No eye has seen it, no ear has heard it, no heart has imagined it (Sahih al-Bukhari 3244)

So ask Allah for Al-Firdaws, the highest level of Jannah (Sahih al-Bukhari 2790) 🤍

O Allah, grant us Al-Firdaws. Ameen.
