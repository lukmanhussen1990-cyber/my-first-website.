# Cubicle Wall: 9:16 3D comedy short (Blender + bpy + ffmpeg)

`final.mp4` is 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC. Its length follows your voiceover. The captions are burned in, and quiet CC0 sound effects sit 18 dB under your voice.

Everything is generated by scripts, the same pipeline as `../chair-sniff`: characters, the cubicle office, the drafting-stool gags, cameras, render, captions and effects.

## What's in this folder

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `final.mp4` | The finished short |
| `cubicle_wall.blend` | The Blender scene: 19 shots, one camera per shot bound to timeline markers. Sketchfab-model textures are packed. Poly Haven and MakeHuman textures load from `assets/`: run `scripts/fetch_assets.py assets` once. |
| `captions/captions.ass` | Captions (Poppins ExtraBold, 1–3 words, hard cut on/off) |
| `voiceover.mp3` | Your voiceover (used untouched) |
| `script.txt` | Your narration, one line per shot group (13 lines), for shot timing and caption text |
| `build/sfx.wav` | The sound-effects bed mixed under the voice |
| `assets_manifest.json` / `credits.txt` | Every third-party asset with source, author and licence |
| `make_video.sh` | Runs the whole pipeline |

## The drafting stool

It's assembled only from ready-made parts:
- A gas-lift bar stool supplies the cylinder (which telescopes when he launches), the lever, the seat, the backrest and the round base. The base is shown as black plastic and cracks in two.
- An office chair supplies the chrome 5-star steel base.
- A metal-rings model supplies the chrome foot ring.

## Re-render with a new voiceover

```bash
scripts/setup.sh                       # once: venv with bpy 5.2 + faster-whisper, installs MPFB2
cp /path/to/voiceover.mp3 voiceover.mp3
./make_video.sh                        # first run: downloads assets, builds characters, renders, encodes
STEPS="transcribe timing scene render sfx encode" ./make_video.sh   # later voiceover changes
```

- Every shot is a fraction of its narration line, so the timing follows the voice automatically. The "Genius!" cut snaps to that word.
- Rendering takes about 7 s per frame on a 4-core CPU with no GPU. Existing frames in `build/frames` are skipped, so a stopped render resumes.
- To tweak a shot, open `cubicle_wall.blend` in Blender 5.2+, or edit `scripts/build_scene.py` and rebuild.

## Assets and licences

- **Characters:** MakeHuman system assets (CC0) through MPFB2: the main guy, the crush, the boss and the little cousin.
- **Office dressing, textures, HDRI:** Poly Haven (CC0).
- **Cubicle partitions, plastic chairs, stool parts, chairs, monitors, whiteboard, water cooler:** Sketchfab models under CC-BY 4.0, downloaded through the public Objaverse mirror. They are credited in `credits.txt`.
- **Sound effects:** CC0 from SFXMint (AI-generated) and wyzowl sfx-kit (synthesised), both on Hugging Face.
- **Animation:** keyframed stiff, Mixamo-style body poses on the MPFB2 *Mixamo* skeleton. Mixamo itself needs an Adobe login.
