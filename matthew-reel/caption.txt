Everyone hated him. Jesus pointed at him anyway.

Caravaggio hid two secrets in "The Calling of Saint Matthew" (1600): a hand borrowed from Michelangelo's Adam, and a light that doesn't come from the window. The God who made the first man was giving a hated tax collector a new start.

He's still saying it: "Follow me." (Matthew 9:9)
