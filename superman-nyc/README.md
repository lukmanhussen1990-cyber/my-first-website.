# WHAT IF SUPERMAN ATTACKED NEW YORK? — 40-second found-footage short

A fan-made, fictional 3D short that looks like a phone video shot on a Manhattan street when Superman
turns hostile. Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, exactly 40.000 s, H.264 + AAC:
**[`superman_nyc_40s.mp4`](superman_nyc_40s.mp4)**.

Everything in the picture is generated by the code in this folder: Blender (bpy) scene building,
simulations in numpy, Cycles rendering, and a Python compositor for the phone and CCTV looks. The
soundtrack is synthesized in numpy and layered with a few ElevenLabs sound effects.

> Fan work, not affiliated with or endorsed by DC. Superman and the "S" shield are trademarks of
> DC Comics. The emblem here is a simplified drawing (`tools/gen_textures.py`). No real people or
> events are depicted, and there is no gore.

All times, positions and trajectories live in [`timeline.py`](timeline.py). Every other script
reads them from there, so picture, effects and sound stay in sync.

## Pipeline

Run the steps in order. Blender is 4.5 LTS. Python needs numpy, scipy, Pillow and OpenEXR.

```bash
# 2D textures: shop signs, interiors, street signs, the emblem
python3 tools/gen_textures.py /opt/work/assets/gen
# rigged, clothed and animated pedestrian library
blender -b --factory-startup -P blender/humans_build.py -- /opt/work/assets/humans.blend
# the hero (also writes the cape attachment frames)
blender -b --factory-startup -P blender/superman.py -- /opt/work/assets/superman.blend
python3 sim/cape_sim.py /opt/work/cache
# the full scene: city, tower, props, traffic, crowd, cyclist, cameras, FX
blender -b --factory-startup -P blender/build_scene.py -- /opt/work/scene.blend
# render (multilayer EXR, resumable); frame ranges can be split across processes
blender -b /opt/work/scene.blend -P blender/render_shots.py -- /opt/work/frames/final 1.0 3 1
# camera track for the rolling-shutter simulation
blender -b --factory-startup -P blender/export_camera.py -- /opt/work/cache/camera_track.json
# soundtrack (SFX_DIR holds the optional ElevenLabs takes as <name>.wav)
python3 audio/sound_design.py SFX_DIR /opt/work/audio/soundtrack.wav
# post-production and encode
python3 post/composite.py /opt/work/frames/final --mp4 superman_nyc_40s.mp4 --audio /opt/work/audio/soundtrack.wav
```

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `blender/city.py`, `tower.py`, `props.py` | Procedural Manhattan intersection: streets and markings, the bike lane, modular facades with glass shaders and interiors, storefronts, signals, street furniture. The hero tower has a facade band that breaks away. |
| `blender/humans_build.py`, `people.py`, `crowd.py` | CC0 human base meshes rigged to a Mixamo skeleton, with clothing shells and retargeted walk/run/idle clips. 66 scripted pedestrians with per-shot visibility culling. |
| `blender/vehicles.py`, `cyclist.py` | Lofted cars, taxis and a bus with speed profiles and brake lights. A bicycle whose rider pedals by two-bone IK. |
| `blender/superman.py`, `sim/cape_sim.py` | The hero: a bulked-up base mesh with costume regions, poses along the flight spline, and a PBD cloth cape. |
| `blender/fx.py`, `sim/fx_sim.py` | Heat vision, Voronoi-fractured glass and concrete, sparks, paper, fire, fireballs, the smoke plume, the dust front, and sun shafts. Also the signal flicker and the bent pole. |
| `blender/cameras.py` | Handheld phone simulation (aim lag, shake, gait, zoom, the fumble, the drop) and the CCTV camera. |
| `post/composite.py` | Phone AE, AgX tone mapping, haze, bloom, local tone mapping, chromatic aberration, rolling shutter from the real camera motion, autofocus hunts, noise and glitches. CCTV look with overlay. Captions. ffmpeg encode. |
| `audio/sound_design.py` | City bed, Doppler flybys rendered from the flight path, boom and echoes, destruction, crowd, breathing and handling. Phone-mic AGC and clipping, the CCTV muffle, a low-end bus, a restrained score, and tinnitus. Normalized to −15 LUFS. |

## Third-party material

- Textures and HDRI: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0).
- Human bodies: Blender Studio "Human Base Meshes" bundle (CC0).
- Animation clips: three.js example `Xbot.glb` (Mixamo rig).
- Sound effects: 17 short clips generated with ElevenLabs: crowd reactions, street ambience, glass,
  sonic boom, flyby, heat beam, impact, explosion, debris, tyre screech, phone drop, breathing,
  running footsteps, car alarm, sparks and a braam. The rest is synthesized, and every cue has a
  synthesized fallback in `sound_design.py`.
- Fonts: Inter (OFL) and DejaVu Sans Mono.
