My town doesn't have toilets. Not one. Not even a tiny one. Every morning at 6 AM, the Bucket Man walks down our street. "BUCKETS OUT!" And everyone runs outside with their bucket. Every house has a bucket. Big families have five. At school, we have a class called "How To Hold It." Our teacher can hold it for three whole days. She's a legend. Everyone thinks buckets are normal. Everyone except me. I keep asking, "Isn't there something better?" So the kids call me Weird Bucket Boy. But one night, I heard Grandpa talking in his sleep. "The Golden Throne... the Golden Throne..." The next morning, I asked him about it. Grandpa closed the curtains, looked around... and whispered: "Long ago, there was a magic seat. You sit. You go. And with one push of a button... WHOOSH. It's gone. Forever." "Where is it?!" "In the old castle on top of Stinky Hill. Nobody who went there ever came back... because nobody can hold it that long." So I packed my bag. Snacks. A flashlight. And my bucket... just in case. I walked for two days. I crossed the Swamp of Smells. I climbed Stinky Hill. And there, inside the castle, under all the dust and spiderwebs... was the Golden Throne. I pressed the button. WHOOSH. I cried a little. I dragged it all the way home. It took four days. I used it twice on the way. When I got back, the whole town was waiting. Grandpa went first. He sat down... pressed the button... WHOOSH. Nobody said anything. Then Grandpa started crying. Then EVERYONE started crying. Oh, and the castle basement? It had a thousand more. Now every house has a Golden Throne. The Bucket Man runs a toilet shop. And the kids don't call me Weird Bucket Boy anymore. They call me... the Flush King.
