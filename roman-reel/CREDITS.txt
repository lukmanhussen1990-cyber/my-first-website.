Roman Toilet Love Story - asset credits
=======================================
Everything used is free and CC0 (public domain) or made from scratch for this reel.
No login-gated downloads were used.

Voice
-----
- voiceover.mp3 - supplied by @garden_reels (ElevenLabs), used as-is (only loudness-normalised to -16 LUFS).

Characters (MakeHuman / MPFB2 for Blender - CC0 system assets)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Base human mesh, default rig, ARKit face units, high-poly eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes,
  hair "short02" and "short04" - MakeHuman Community / MPFB2 system assets (CC0).
- Tunics (white with red clavi, ochre), belts, strapped sandals, heart eyes, blush and soot
  layers - generated procedurally from the body mesh by our own scripts.

Set (Roman public latrine)
--------------------------
- Mosaic floor and Pompeian-style fresco walls - painted procedurally by our own script (texgen.py).
- Poly Haven (CC0): HDRI "kloofendal" (clamped); textures marble_01, castle_wall_slates,
  white_rough_plaster, clay_roof_tiles_02, dark_wooden_planks.
- Keyhole marble bench, water gutter, consoles, roof, lamp shelves - modelled procedurally.

Props
-----
- Tersorium (sponge on a stick), love note ("AMO TE"), scroll, heart bubbles, amphora,
  oil lamps - modelled procedurally by our own scripts (Poly Haven CC0 textures where textured).

  0912, 0913, 0987, 1223, 1340, 1341, 1384, 1386, 1695, 1696, 2284, 3094).
