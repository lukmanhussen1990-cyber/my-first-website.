# ArrowGO! icons

These are the brand assets for ArrowGO! by ImranO. The logo is a rounded-square tile with a deep indigo to royal-blue to teal diagonal gradient (`#2B2672` to `#1F4E96` to `#0F9C98`, top-left to bottom-right, with a soft top sheen and a faint inner rim). On it, three nested L-shaped arrows in coral `#FF7A5C`, amber `#FFB93B` and cream `#F5EBD8` wind toward the centre like a small maze; they have thick rounded strokes, solid heads and a darker depth edge. They go with the app palette: cream `#F5EBD8`, cocoa `#5C3F26`, terracotta `#C77A3A` and sky-blue `#4DA3F0`. `logo.svg` is the master (viewBox 512, tile `rx` 112). It is structured for animation: `rect.logo-tile`, then `g.logo-arrows`, which carries the depth filter, then three `g.logo-arrow-group.arrow-1|2|3`. Each group holds a `path.logo-shaft`, whose `pathLength="1"` lets you draw it in with `stroke-dasharray: 1 1` as `stroke-dashoffset` goes from 1 to 0, and a `path.logo-head`. `logo-mark.svg` has the same arrows on a transparent background and uses its own IDs, so it can be inlined next to `logo.svg`. `favicon.svg` is a flat version with thicker strokes for tiny sizes, and `favicon-32.png` is its 32 px raster. `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` are the rounded tile on transparency (manifest purpose `any`). `maskable-512.png` is a full-bleed gradient with the arrows inside the central 80% safe circle (purpose `maskable`). `apple-touch-icon.png` is 180×180, full-bleed and opaque. `og-image.png` is the 1200×630 social card: cream background, the logo, and the "ArrowGO!" wordmark with "by ImranO" set in Nunito. The Android launcher set lives in `../android/res/`: legacy `ic_launcher` and `ic_launcher_round` mipmaps, plus an adaptive icon made of the gradient `drawable/ic_launcher_background.xml` and an `ic_launcher_foreground` layer that also serves as the monochrome themed icon.
