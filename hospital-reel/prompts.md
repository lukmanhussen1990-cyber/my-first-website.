## How to make the images
1. Make the **character sheet** first (prompt below).
2. For every shot: upload the character sheet to ChatGPT, then paste the shot prompt. Each prompt already contains the STYLE block.
3. **Shot 03b is special:** upload your finished **shot03a** and say:
   *"keep everything the same, only add the lump, stain, stink lines and shocked eyes"*
4. Name the files `shot01.png`, `shot02.png` … (or just send them in order). For the ★ shots you can also send a Kling clip.
5. No text in any image. I add the captions, the calendar numbers and the handwriting with code.

**STYLE block (same in every prompt):**
> Low-poly blocky 3D render, PS1 / Roblox style, slightly pixelated textures, simple blocky characters with square heads and simple faces with dot eyes, soft realistic lighting, bright and clean colors, silly and cute (never scary). Vertical portrait image, subject in the center, no text.

**Place:** bright, clean, modern clinic: white walls, light wood, big windows with sunshine, green plants, light-blue waiting-room chairs. Other people are normal, calm, friendly adults (reading, playing cards, watering plants). No beds, no straitjackets, no scary details.

**The lump:** from shot 03b on, whenever YOU is seen from the side or back, the prompt says to show *a big round cartoon lump in the back of his jeans (like a balloon), a small brown stain and green wavy stink lines*. Keep it in.

★ = the 6 most important shots. Kling prompt underneath, if you want to make a clip.
