Q1.What is a preposition? How many types of prepositions are there?

Ans.

A preposition is a word that shows the relationship between a noun and a pronoun or pronoun and some other word in a sentence.

Example

The book is on the table.

on shows how the book is related to the table.

What I corrected

7 fixes

Turn on Corrections at the top to see each fix marked inside the notes.

  1. Meaning · definition

    a noun and a pronoun and some other word→changed to a noun or pronoun and some other word

    A preposition links one noun or pronoun to another word. It doesn’t need both a noun and a pronoun.

  2. Meaning · type 2

    tell us about the place of an action→changed to tell us where someone or something is, or where an action takes place

    They also show where things are when there is no action, e.g. The book is on the table.

  3. Grammar · type 3

    Preposition of direction and movement are used…→changed to Prepositions of direction and movement are used…

    The verb are needs a plural subject.

  4. Grammar · heading 2

    Preposition of place→changed to Prepositions of place

    Plural, to match the other two headings.

  5. Spelling · place examples

    infront of→changed to in front of

    Always written as three separate words.

  6. Punctuation · direction examples

    out, of→changed to out of

    Out of is one two-word preposition, so there’s no comma inside it.

  7. Spelling & punctuation · everywhere

    E.g.:-→changed to E.g.

    Spellings tidied up, and missing commas and full stops added throughout.

Checked and kept as written: “There are three types of prepositions.” Time, place and movement/direction are the three main types taught in school. Bigger grammar books also list others, like prepositions of agent (by) and instrument (with). Every example word in your lists is correct for its type.
