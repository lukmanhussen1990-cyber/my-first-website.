Ember & Oat: our brand story

Prepared with Claude · Updated today

Who we are

We’re a neighborhood roastery for people who like their mornings slow and their coffee bright. Every bean is traceable, every cup is made by someone who knows your name.

Voice & tone

  • Warm, never fussy — talk like a regular, not a sommelier.
  • Specific over clever: origin, roast day, tasting notes.
  • Invite people in; don’t tell them what to like.
“Good coffee is a small ritual. We just make it a little easier to keep.”

Who we’re for

Early risers, remote workers, and weekend regulars who want a third place that feels like home — and coffee worth slowing down for.
