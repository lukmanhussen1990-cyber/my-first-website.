How to play
  • Place the striker on your baseline with the slider, or drag along the baseline.
  • Pull back anywhere and release to shoot. The dotted guide shows where the striker meets its first coin.
  • Classic: White breaks. Pocket all nine of your coins. Sinking your own coin or the queen gives you another shot.
  • The queen (red) must be covered: pocket one of your coins on the same or the next shot, or she goes back to the centre.
  • Foul: pocketing the striker ends your turn. Coins from that shot return and you pay one due coin from your tray.
  • Your last coin can't go in before the queen is covered.
  • Freestyle: any coin scores. White 20, black 10, queen 50 once covered. First to 160 wins.

You win!

Won by 4 coins with the queen.
