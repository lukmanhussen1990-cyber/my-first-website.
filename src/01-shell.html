How to Play

Movement & Combat

Roam the wilds and strike with three weapons. Sword sweeps a fast arc, Spear thrusts far and pierces, Wand hurls seeking bolts. Attacks and dashes burn stamina — it refills quickly when you hold back.

Monsters

Passive creatures wander harmlessly, but strike one and its whole temper turns. Aggressive beasts hunt you on sight. Elites wear crowns, hit hard, and pay well.

Growing Stronger

Every kill feeds XP. Level up to pick one of three boons. Find obelisks to temper a weapon a full tier, and shrines to heal. Chain kills quickly for an XP multiplier.
