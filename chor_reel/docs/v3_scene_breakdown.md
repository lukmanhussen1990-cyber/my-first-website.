# v3: The Buddha Version: Scene Breakdown

Format: 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 1,591 frames, 53.14 s. The **original audio track** is stream-copied, unchanged.

## What changed from v2 (from the feedback)

| Feedback | Fix in v3 |
|---|---|
| Not in sync with the song | Every item appears on its **key word** and is taken into the moon exactly on **"ले जा"**, using word timings measured from the original audio (`tools/v3/v3_beats.py`). After each "ले जा", a thin stream runs from the moon into the thief's hand. Cuts land on the start of each lyric line. The house owner's lips follow the singing voice. |
| Characters too stiff | The house owner is a new rigged character. His arms are re-painted every frame, so there are no joint seams. He offers with open palms, stretches out and bows on every "ले जा", raises his hand, looks up at the moon, breathes and sways. The thief really walks in, beckons as each gift reaches him, and lowers his hand at the end. |
| Glitches and ghosting | No cross-dissolves between camera angles: hard cuts only. No sprite-to-sprite crossfades. The room turns into the night sky through one in-camera effect, a starry dome. |
| Not like the original | Same layout as the original room: an arched moon window, brass scales in an arched niche, the owner sitting on a low wooden seat with a small clay lamp in front of him, and his huge lamp shadow on the wall. The same items are taken in the same order and on the same words. The ending matches the original: a starry body, letters turning into birds, the lamp going out, the thief bursting into light, star pictures and the title. |
| The river disappeared | The river stays: the door shot now has the backwater, the ghat steps and the moored boat below the verandah, and the room's window looks out on the river. |
| Use a Buddha (not fat) as the house owner | The house owner is drawn from scratch as a slim, serene Buddha in the Kerala painting style. He has snail-curl hair with the ushnisha, long earlobes and downcast closed eyes. He wears a saffron robe over one shoulder and sits cross-legged on a low seat. His gestures include the abhaya ("fear not") mudra on "डर". |

The thief is still the round-bellied man from the Kerala video, in his natural colours.
