Signs before the Last Day 🌙

As told by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ:

• Shepherds will compete in building tall towers
• Knowledge will be taken away
• Earthquakes will increase
• Time will pass quickly
• People will boast about their mosques
• Trust will be lost
• Wealth will overflow, and no one will take charity
• Arabia will become green with rivers
• The Euphrates will uncover a mountain of gold
• Then the 10 great signs will come
• The sun will rise from the West

Repent before the sun rises from the West.
May Allah protect us. Ameen 🤲

Sources: Sahih al-Bukhari 50, 59, 1036, 4635, 7119 · Sahih Muslim 157, 2703, 2901 · Sunan Abi Dawud 449
