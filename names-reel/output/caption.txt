9 Beautiful Names of Allah ✨ Part 1
أسماء الله الحسنى

1. Ar-Razzaq – The Provider · الرَّزَّاق
2. As-Sami' – The All-Hearing · السَّمِيع
3. Al-Basir – The All-Seeing · البَصِير
4. Al-Latif – The Subtle, The Kind · اللَّطِيف
5. Al-Ghafur – The Forgiving · الغَفُور
6. An-Nur – The Light · النُّور
7. Al-Hafiz – The Protector · الحَفِيظ
8. Al-Wadud – The Most Loving · الوَدُود
9. Al-Qarib – The Near · القَرِيب

"To Allah belong the most beautiful Names, so call on Him by them" (Surah Al-A'raf 7:180)
