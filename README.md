# my-first-website.

A tiny site featuring **Clawd**, the coral-orange 8-bit mascot.

## Clawd — Dancing Heart Loop

`index.html` is a self-contained, seamless looping pixel-art animation:

1. Clawd faces the camera and does a happy bouncy dance — tiny hops, side-to-side
   steps, wiggles, and squash-&-stretch.
2. He spins 180° to turn his back to the camera.
3. A bright pink pixel heart is revealed on his back, sparkling in.
4. The heart softly pulses and glows while Clawd does little happy bounces.
5. He spins back around and settles into the exact starting pose — so the loop
   is perfect.

Turn on **Sound** for an original Web Audio chiptune tune with tiny boings on the
jumps, a pixel whoosh on each turn, and a sparkle when the heart appears.

No build step, no dependencies — just open `index.html` in a browser. Clawd's
front and back share one data-driven pixel grid, so his shape, colour, square
eyes, four legs and retro design stay identical in every frame.
