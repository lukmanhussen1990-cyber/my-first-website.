# my-first-website.

## rain-walk.html

A self-contained canvas animation: two men in silhouette walking down a wet city
street at night in the rain. Open the file in any browser — no build step, no
dependencies, everything (markup, styles, script) lives in the one HTML file.

- Procedurally animated walk cycles (two-bone IK on legs and arms) so the stride,
  arm swing and body bob stay in sync with the scrolling street.
- Layered rain with depth: far drops are faint and slow, near drops fast and
  bright, and drops passing a street lamp catch its light.
- Splash ripples on the road, water shedding off the umbrella rims, drifting mist,
  parallax skyline with flickering windows, and reflections on the wet asphalt.
- Lightning strikes every few seconds — click anywhere to trigger one.

Resizes with the window and scales down to phone-sized viewports.
