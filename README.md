## Second Sight — Minecraft Bedrock addon

`minecraft-addon/` holds a Minecraft **Bedrock (mobile)** addon — behaviour
pack + resource pack — that adds **survival god mode** and a **second-person
camera** (the view looks *at* your character instead of out of their eyes).

- Ready-to-install file: **`dist/SecondSightGodMode.mcaddon`** — download it to
  your phone and tap it.
- Full instructions, command list and design notes:
  [`minecraft-addon/README.md`](minecraft-addon/README.md)

Targets Minecraft for Android v1.21.0.26. No experimental toggles, no Beta
APIs, no scripting — vanilla `/camera` and `/effect` commands only.
