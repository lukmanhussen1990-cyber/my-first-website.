What Are Microorganisms?

Microorganisms (also called microbes or germs) are living things so small that you cannot see them with just your eyes — you need a microscope. They live everywhere: in water, soil, air, food, and even inside your own body. Some can make you sick, but most are harmless, and many are actually helpful — they help you digest food, make bread rise, and keep nature in balance.
