Block Blast 1.1.0 — 8x8 block puzzle for Android 8.0+ (signed release APK).

Download **BlockBlast.apk** below and open it on your phone to install
(allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/file manager if asked).
It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your best score, settings and saved game.

What's new:

- Redesigned home screen: animated logo, BEST badge, Continue / How to
  Play / Block Skins, About, a gold **PREMIUM** button and the
  "CREATED BY IMRAN" credit. The app now opens on the home screen.
- **Premium** subscriptions through Google Play (Yearly, Monthly, Weekly)
  with No Ads, More Revives (3 per game), Hint Boosts (5 per game) and the
  Candy, Neon and Gem block skins. Purchases are verified before anything
  unlocks; Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription included.
- Free play: 1 hint and 1 ad-funded revive per game, banner and
  occasional interstitial ads (this build uses Google's test ads).
- Snappier piece pickup, landing flash and dust, haptic feedback, a shake
  on big clears and new screen transitions.

Subscriptions can only be bought once the app is set up in Google Play
Console (see docs/MONETIZATION.md in the repository); in this build the
Premium screen shows reference prices with purchasing turned off.

- Package: `com.myapps.blockblast`
- Min Android: 8.0 (API 26)
- Game Creator: IMRAN
