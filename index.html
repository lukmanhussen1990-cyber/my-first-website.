The Long Road to Freedom

Nearly two centuries of resistance, sacrifice and non-violent struggle led to the midnight of 15 August 1947.

1857

The First War of Independence

A nationwide revolt against the East India Company became the first mass challenge to colonial rule.

1885

Indian National Congress Founded

A political platform is born, giving the freedom movement a unified national voice.

1919

Jallianwala Bagh

The massacre at Amritsar turned moderate opinion decisively toward complete independence.

1930

The Dandi Salt March

Gandhi walked 386 km to the sea and made a fistful of salt the symbol of defiance.

1942

Quit India Movement

"Do or Die." The final mass call for the British to leave India.

1947

Freedom at Midnight

At the stroke of the midnight hour of 15 August, India awoke to life and freedom.

"At the stroke of the midnight hour, when the world sleeps, India will awake to life and freedom." — Jawaharlal Nehru, 14 August 1947

Those Who Gave Everything

A nation remembers the leaders and martyrs whose courage made this day possible.

🕊️

Mahatma Gandhi

Led the freedom struggle through truth and non-violence, inspiring movements worldwide.

🔥

Bhagat Singh

Gave his life at 23, turning youth across India toward the cause of complete freedom.

⚔️

Subhas Chandra Bose

Founded the Indian National Army with the cry, "Give me blood, and I will give you freedom."

🛡️

Rani Lakshmibai

The Queen of Jhansi who fought to the last in 1857 and became a symbol of resistance.

🏛️

Sardar Patel

The Iron Man who united 562 princely states into one republic.

📜

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Architect of the Constitution that guaranteed liberty, equality and justice to all.

The National Pledge

India is my country and all Indians are my brothers and sisters.
I love my country and I am proud of its rich and varied heritage.
I shall always strive to be worthy of it.
I shall give my parents, teachers and all elders respect and treat everyone with courtesy.
To my country and my people, I pledge my devotion.
