An arcade game about the greenest chemistry on Earth

CHLOROPLAST

You are a single chloroplast inside a living leaf. Sunlight pours from above, water climbs from the roots, carbon dioxide drifts in through the stomata. Turn all three into sugar — before the leaf runs out of breath.

1Drink water. Blue droplets rise from the xylem. Fill your tank to 6.
2Catch photons. Each one splits a stored H₂O into ATP energy + a puff of O₂. No water, no split.
3Fix carbon. Grey CO₂ molecules drift in from the air. Collect 6.
46 ATP + 6 CO₂ = glucose. The Calvin cycle fires automatically and the leaf revives.
Move mouse or drag to steer W A S D / arrows P pause
