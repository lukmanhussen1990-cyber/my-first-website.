The premise

Teachers lost their authority.
The state sent someone to take it back.

School violence has outrun the people meant to stop it — teachers can't discipline, parents won't accept blame, and the law arrives too late to matter. So the Ministry of Education stands up a new agency: the Educational Rights Protection Bureau, authorised to enter any school and use physical intervention. Inspector Na Hwa-jin, a former Special Forces captain, works school by school through bullying, gang extortion, cyber defamation, stalking, drugs and gambling — and finds that the students are rarely the real problem.

Critical consensus

The counter-argument, worth reading before you binge: reviewers have accused the series of glamorising vigilante justice — dressing “might makes right” in superhero packaging — and one sequence involving false accusations drew criticism over its choice of filming location. Director Hong Jong-chan has said the adaptation deliberately shifted weight from the webtoon's spectacle toward supporting victims.

From webtoon to Netflix

How it got here

  • Source

    Get Schooled (참교육) on Naver

    Chae Yong-taek and Han Ga-ram's webtoon builds the ERPB premise — and the controversy about its methods — long before the cameras roll.

  • Adaptation

    Ylab Plex & GTist take it to series

    Hong Jong-chan directs; Lee Nam-kyu, Kim Da-hee and Moon Jong-ho write. Kim Mu-yeol, Lee Sung-min, Jin Ki-joo and Pyo Ji-hoon head the cast.

  • 5 June 2026

    All 10 episodes drop on Netflix

    No weekly wait. The series goes straight to number one on the global non-English chart.

  • Shared universe

    Same world as Study Group

    Both adapted from the same webtoon lineage — a school-action universe that keeps growing.
