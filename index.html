HOW TO PLAY

Goal: find the 5 notes pinned around the maze, then find the EXIT door that appears. Notes crackle with radio static and the exit hums — use your ears (headphones help), or watch the STATIC meter under the notes counter.

WASD / arrows move   Mouse look   Shift run   C crouch   F flashlight   Space hold breath   E close note   Esc pause
Gamepad: left stick move, right stick look, RT run, RB hold breath, A crouch, X flashlight, Start pause.
Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb drags to look, buttons for run / crouch / light / breath.

Something hunts you. It hears running from far away, walking from closer, crouching barely at all. It sees you better when you stand in the light or use the flashlight. If it spots you, break its line of sight, get into the dark, crouch and wait. When it is right next to you, hold your breath — but not for too long. When the lights start to stutter, it is close.

Sometimes it just stands and watches. Stare back and it may slip away… or it may not. Never walk towards it.

Running drains stamina. The flashlight drains its battery — spare batteries lie on the floor. A rare bottle of almond water refills your stamina and gives you a second wind.

Modes: escape Level 1 to unlock Level 2 (flooded) and Endless; escape Level 2 to unlock Level 3 (lights out). Settings has difficulties from Explore (no creature) to Hard, plus captions and a Reduce-flashing option.
