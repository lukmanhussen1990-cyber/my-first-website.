Chapter I

What Does "Dajjal" Mean?

He is known as Al-Masih ad-Dajjal, the False Messiah. His name comes from an Arabic root meaning to cover over, to gild or to deceive: he hides the truth under a false shine.

المَسِيحAl-Masih"The Messiah". He falsely claims this title, which truly belongs to 'Isa (Jesus), peace be upon him.
دَجَلَDajala"To cover, to smear over". Like gold plating over base metal.
الكَذَّابThe LiarHis whole mission is a lie. In the end he will even claim to be God.
T R U T H

He covers the truth, but only for those who do not know it.

Chapter II

Every Prophet Warned of Him

From Nuh (Noah) onward, every prophet warned their people about him. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ spoke of him more than anyone, and taught that no trial from the creation of Adam until the Hour is greater.

"There was no prophet who did not warn his nation about the one-eyed liar." (Sahih al-Bukhari 7131, Sahih Muslim 2933) "Between the creation of Adam and the Hour there is no creation greater than the Dajjal." (Sahih Muslim 2946)
Chapter III

How He Will Be Recognised

  • Blind in the right eyeHis right eye is blind, looking like a floating grape. (Bukhari 3439, Muslim 169)
  • "Kafir" written between his eyesEvery believer will be able to read it, whether they can read or not. (Muslim 2933)
  • A young man with very curly hairHeavily built, reddish in complexion. (Bukhari 7128, Muslim 2937)
  • Your Lord is not one-eyedThe Prophet ﷺ gave this as the plainest proof that he is a fraud: a flawed creature cannot be God. (Bukhari 7131)
Chapter IV

Where He Will Appear

He will come out on a road between Sham (Greater Syria) and Iraq, spreading mischief right and left, and will travel the earth with the speed of rain driven by the wind.

(Sahih Muslim 2937: the hadith of an-Nawwas ibn Sam'an)

Chapter VIII

The End of the Deceiver

Allah will send down 'Isa ibn Maryam (Jesus, son of Mary), peace be upon him, at the white minaret east of Damascus, his hands resting on the wings of two angels.

'Isa pursues him and catches him at the Gate of Lud, where he kills him. On seeing 'Isa, the Dajjal begins to dissolve like salt dissolving in water.

(Sahih Muslim 2937, 2897)
Chapter IX

How to Be Protected

The Prophet ﷺ did not only warn. He also gave clear means of protection.

01

Surah Al-Kahf

Memorise the first ten verses of Surah Al-Kahf.

ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنزَلَ عَلَىٰ عَبْدِهِ ٱلْكِتَٰبَ وَلَمْ يَجْعَل لَّهُۥ عِوَجَا

"Whoever memorises ten verses from the beginning of Surah Al-Kahf will be protected from the Dajjal." (Muslim 809)
02

Seek Refuge in Every Prayer

After the final tashahhud, before salam:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ جَهَنَّمَ، وَمِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ، وَمِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَحْيَا وَالْمَمَاتِ، وَمِنْ شَرِّ فِتْنَةِ الْمَسِيحِ الدَّجَّالِ

"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of Hell, the punishment of the grave, the trials of life and death, and the evil of the trial of the False Messiah." (Muslim 588)
03

Keep Away From Him

Do not go to him out of curiosity or confidence. A man will go to him thinking he is a firm believer, then follow him because of the doubts he stirs up.

(Abu Dawud 4319)
04

Know Your Lord

True knowledge of Allah is the strongest shield. Allah is not seen in this world, is not one-eyed, and is unlike His creation. Any creature who claims divinity is a liar.

"You will not see your Lord until you die." (Muslim 169)
وَاللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ

And Allah knows best.
