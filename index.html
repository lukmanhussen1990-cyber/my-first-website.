The Invitation

Circle. Triangle. Square.

Three shapes rule the island — printed on the card, stitched onto every mask, carved into the whole machine.

○ The Worker

The lowest rank of masked staff — the hands that move bodies, serve food and keep the games running without ever speaking.

△ The Soldier

Armed enforcers who guard the players and carry out eliminations. They outrank workers but answer to the circles above.

□ The Manager

The highest-ranking masks on the field, commanding rooms full of soldiers and reporting only to the Front Man himself.

The Game

Debt In. Death Out.

Hundreds of people crushed by debt accept a stranger's invitation and wake on a secret island. Each round is a children's game — and every loss is fatal. The pot swells with every death, and the last one standing walks away with ₩45.6 billion. In Season 2, one of them came back on purpose: Player 456, Seong Gi-hun, who took his winnings and swore to end the game from the inside.
