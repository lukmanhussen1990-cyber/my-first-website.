The reminder

How big is Allah's mercy?

The Prophet ﷺ said: Allah made mercy in one hundred parts.

He sent down only one part to the earth.

And from that one part… comes all the love in the world.

A mother hugging her child. A bird feeding her chicks. A cat carrying her kitten.

Even a horse lifts her hoof… so she doesn't hurt her baby.

All of that… is just one part.

The other ninety-nine… Allah kept with Him.

For His servants… on the Day of Judgement.

So never lose hope. Allah says: do not despair of the mercy of Allah.

Sources

Hadith

“Allah divided mercy into one hundred parts. He kept ninety-nine parts with Him and sent down one part to the earth, and because of that one single part His creations are merciful to each other, so that even the mare lifts her hoof away from her young, lest she should trample on it.”

Narrated by Abu Hurairah · Sahih al-Bukhari 6000; see also Sahih Muslim 2752

Qur'an

“Say, ‘O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful.’”
