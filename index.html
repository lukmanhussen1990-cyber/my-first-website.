How to play

🎭
Move the voidDrag anywhere on the screen. The void follows your finger.
🍩
Swallow thingsRoll over anything smaller than your rim. Each bite grows you.
🏢
Grow, then feastCones and bins first, then cars, houses, buses and finally skyscrapers.
⚠
Watch the glowObjects glow green when you can eat them, red when they are still too big.
👻
Rival voidsEat smaller rivals whole. Run from anything bigger than you.
⚡
Power-upsGrab floating orbs for speed, magnet pull, double score or a shield.
🔥
CombosKeep swallowing without pausing to stack a score multiplier.
⏱
Beat the clockHighest score when the timer hits zero takes the city.
⌚
DesktopMouse to steer · WASD or arrow keys · Space to start / restart · Esc to pause
📱
MobileDrag anywhere, or switch on the virtual joystick in Settings
