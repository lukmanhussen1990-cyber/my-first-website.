SCP-096

"The Shy Guy" — Minecraft Bedrock addon (BP + RP)

EUCLID · KEEP OUT OF SIGHT
⬇ Download SCP-096.mcaddon Behavior pack + resource pack — tap to open in Minecraft
Behavior Pack only Resource Pack only

HOW TO INSTALL (Android / iOS)

  1. Tap the big red button above.
  2. When the download finishes, tap the downloaded file.
  3. Minecraft opens and imports both packs automatically.
  4. Create or edit a world → Behavior Packs → activate SCP-096. The resource pack switches on with it.
  5. In the world, open the creative inventory and search SCP-096 — the spawn egg is under Items / Spawn Eggs.

THE SPAWN EGG

  • Name: Spawn SCP-096
  • Pale bone-white egg with dried-blood blotches.
  • Get it in survival: /give @s scp:scp_096_spawn_egg
  • Or skip the egg entirely: /summon scp:scp_096
