    Playing well

    • Aim shots by holding a direction as you release — up and down move the ball inside the frame.
    • Steer with left/right while shooting to bend it around the keeper.
    • Through balls are timed, not aimed: play them before the run, not after.
    • Slide tackles in your own box give away penalties. Stand up instead.
    • Sprinting drains stamina; tired players lose a yard of pace.
