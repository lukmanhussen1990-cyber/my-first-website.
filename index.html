HOW TO PLAY

Goal: find the 5 notes pinned around the maze, then find the EXIT door that appears. Notes crackle with radio static and the exit hums — use your ears (headphones help).

WASD / arrows move   Mouse look   Shift run   C crouch   F flashlight   E close note   Esc pause
Phone: left thumb moves, right thumb drags to look, buttons for run / crouch / light.

Something hunts you. It hears running from far away, walking from closer, crouching barely at all. It sees you better when you stand in the light or use the flashlight. If it spots you, break its line of sight, get into the dark, crouch and wait. When the lights start to stutter, it is close.

Running drains stamina. The flashlight drains its battery — spare batteries lie on the floor.
