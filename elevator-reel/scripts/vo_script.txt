You spend all day trying to get it up. But your grandma's piano weighs a ton, and the stairs are destroying your back, so you tie a rope to it and pull it up from the roof. That works, but the rope rips your hands apart, so you hang it over a wheel. Clever! Now you're pulling down instead of up, but it's still crazy heavy, so you tie a giant rock to the other end. Now the rock does half the work, but the piano keeps swinging into the neighbors' windows, so you build a shaft around it. Then you nail a box to the rope so you can ride up with it. But your arms are dead, so you hook up a motor. Now it goes up and down at the push of a button. Congratulations! You finally got it up. You've invented the elevator!
