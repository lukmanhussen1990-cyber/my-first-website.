# Frankenwriter / Imran Typer – everything in it

One file: `index.html` (no images, no audio files, no libraries; only the Courier Prime font from Google Fonts).
The Android app (`ImranTyper.apk`, version 1.3) wraps the same page.

## The machine (rebuilt from the recording, measured frame by frame)
- Typewriter page with the Frankenstein capsule on its pipe: T-bar strike, carriage glide, line wrap with carriage
  return and paper feed, blinking green caret dot, all timed to the recording.
- Electric zaps between the bolts, sparks, steam from the top bolt, green glow and flood, cobwebs that move
  like folded tape (with spiders on threads), measured from the video.
- Lamp mode: the dial turns the lamp on with the recording's timing, a light fan fitted to the video that trails
  the carriage; sliders for light and sound.
- Sound and music synthesized live: key clicks, return clunk, ding, and the recording's music (chords, bass,
  hats, theremin lead), levelled to the recording.

## Skins (tap the capsule, or the control panel)
- Ten skins drawn from real sprites: Frankenstein (the recorded one), Pumpkin, Vampire (with flapping bat
  wings), Mummy, Ghost (floats, see-through), Witch, Claude Special (spark that spins while you type, speech
  bubble), Classic Brass, Steampunk Copper (gauge needle rises while typing), Space Capsule (porthole of stars,
  thruster fires while typing).
- Each has its own spark/flood/steam colours, crackle pitch, pick-up sound and return-bell note; a melting
  cross-fade with a shockwave ring when you switch; remembered between visits.

## Writing tools
- Drafts: new, open, rename, delete with undo, thumbnails of each draft, export; back up / restore everything
  (.json).
- Pages: a full sheet tears itself off and stays with the draft; scroll back through earlier lines and pages
  (drag or mouse wheel, perforation between pages, page label).
- Tear the page off by hand: pull the paper tab up, the sheet stretches, rips free, follows your finger and
  flies off (or springs back if you let go early); Undo.
- Share menu: share text / copy, share as a picture (a 1080 px card of your page with the capsule and a frozen
  spark), save .txt, print / PDF, tear off page.
- Word goal and writing sprint with a celebration (capsule burst, electric lashes, firework rings).
- Margin bell four letters before the end of a line; faster typing strikes lighter.
- Keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+L, Ctrl+S, Ctrl+P, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+comma, Ctrl+Shift+Enter, Ctrl+M, Ctrl+Shift+S, Esc).

## Control panel (the slider icon)
- Styled like an old machine's panel (brass plate, typewriter keys, bakelite switches).
- Mode: Normal, Solar, Full dark, Horror.
- Paper: Cream, Old Parchment, Blueprint, Black (white ink). Ribbon: black, red, blue, sepia.
- Sound pack: Electric Frankenstein, Classic Typewriter, Spooky Halloween (wind, creaks, owl, thunder); volume,
  music on/off, bell, vibration, lamp, goals, shortcut list. Sound cycles all / effects only / off.

## Atmosphere modes
- Solar: window light across the sheet with passing clouds, sunbeams, golden dust, a day sky with sun, clouds,
  hills and castle on wide screens, birds and birdsong; with the lamp on the room goes to dusk.
- Full dark: near-black sheet with soft grey ink, black room, dark vignette.
- Horror: scorched, blood-spattered sheet with blood-red ink, letters that bleed drops, a heartbeat in the dark,
  flickering lights, a shadow passing the lamp, ghost words that type themselves when you stop, red lightning,
  a blood moon and something with red eyes outside; low drone and heartbeat.

## Ambience and graphics
- Rare distant lightning with thunder (lamp dips, webs tremble); bats crossing the page; a moth circling the lamp;
  a spider letting itself down; dust and light shafts in the lamp beam; bulb shimmer.
- Wide screens: a night window behind the machine (moon, stars, hills, castle, dead tree, graves,
  jack-o'-lanterns, fog), dimming when the lamp is on.
- Electric extras: forked arcs, plasma at the bolt heads, lingering afterglow, arcs creeping round the capsule
  and leaping along the pipe, St. Elmo's fire under the lamp, the arc lighting the pipe and paper.
- Torn edges with real rip shapes and fibres, paper scraps tumbling down, the fresh sheet rolling in.
- Every paper, ink and skin checked in light and lamp mode for contrast; toasts and menus in brass and walnut.

## Phones and accessibility
- Works with phone keyboards, autocorrect and predictive text; the keyboard stays up through menus; layout
  adapts down to 320 px and to landscape/desktop.
- Light haptics on Android (keys, return, tear, skin change), silent when the sound is off.
- Reduced motion respected (no flashes, bats, fog or flying pages); keyboard navigation in every menu; screen
  reader labels and announced messages.
- About 4–6 ms per frame on a phone profile at 4x CPU slowdown; heap flat in long soak tests.

## Android app "Imran Typer" (ImranTyper.apk, 1.3)
- Your logo as the app icon (normal, round and adaptive), green splash; works offline (font bundled, no network
  permission).
- Save .txt / pictures / backups into Download and Pictures, Android share sheet, printing, file picker for
  restore, back button closes menus first.
- Opening film (about 9 s, tap to skip), made from your logo picture cut into its moving parts:
  - fade up from black; a sheet fed in by two turns of the knob, the knob ridges rolling, ratchet clicks and
    paper rustle;
  - the return lever swings and the carriage slams to the margin with a line feed, rack buzz, thunk, bounce and
    camera shake;
  - a close-up on the type guide: "IMRAN TYPER" typed with human timing, each key going down, its type bar
    swinging up on a 3D arc, the ribbon lifting, the letter struck into the paper with uneven ink, the carriage
    stepping along; the bell near the margin; the carriage returned again;
  - the paper rolls up and the camera closes in on the typed name, then fades into the app;
  - camera moves, depth of field, lamp light, dust, lens falloff, film grain, motion smear on the fast carriage,
    paper texture, shading and shadow;
  - all sounds synthesized: key thock, slug strike, escapement tick, key release, bell, ratchet, carriage slide
    and margin stop, room tone and reverb; a light phone tap on each strike;
  - lowers its own effects on slow phones so it stays smooth.
