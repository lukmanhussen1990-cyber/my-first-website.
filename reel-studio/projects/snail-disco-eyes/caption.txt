Socho… ek aisa ghongha jiski aankhein dhadak rahi hon 👀🐌

Ye koi horror film nahi hai. Ek chhota sa flatworm (Leucochloridium paradoxum) ghonghe ki aankhon mein ghus kar unhe hari-safed dhariyon wali, dhadakti "disco" thailiyon mein badal deta hai. Scientists maante hain ki chidiya inhe mota rasila caterpillar samajhti hai… aur ek chonch mein parasite apni agli sawari tak pahunch jaata hai. Nature sach mein ajeeb hai 🧬
