# Style notes: reference reel (jewel wasp vs cockroach)

Source: 120x214 InShot export, 24 fps, 139 s, AAC stereo. Frames studied at 1 fps (contact sheets) and 6 fps (key moments), with 4–10x zooms. Below, positions are converted to 1080x1920.

## Format and pacing
- 9:16. A new shot every **1.5–3 s**, cut on voice beats. Almost all cuts are hard cuts.
- Special transitions: an **X-ray scan reveal** (a bright horizontal scan line sweeps down, 6.0 s), a **vertical page-roll** (a paper cylinder sweeps right to left, 76.3 s), and quick whip/blur pushes on some cuts.
- The camera is never still: slow push-ins (about 3–6% over a shot) and Ken Burns drift.

## Two looks
**PAPER**
- Warm field-journal paper: about **#d6c4a6 at the lit centre** to **#c6ad84 mid**, falling to **#ac9970 at the edges** and **#96825f in the corners**. That's a strong vignette with fine grain and fibres.
- Faint vertical crease/column lines at **x ≈ 36 px** and **x ≈ 666 px** (soft darker bands).
- A thin dark rule at **y ≈ 260 px** from x 90 to 980, with tiny typewriter labels just above it: a code on the left ("FILE No. 01") and "FIG. n" on the right.
- Small square tick marks run down the left margin (x ≈ 36, every ~107 px, alternating size).
- Animals are realistic vintage plates in side or top view, standing on an implied ground line with a soft contact shadow.
- Sometimes a stronger spotlight vignette is used (the burrow scenes).

**DARK X-RAY**
- Warm near-black **#1b1b17**, a faint grid (a vertical line at x ≈ 675, faint horizontal lines), small ticks on the left edge.
- On the right edge: a column of small grey squares (x ≈ 1010–1035, every ~62 px, from y 280 to 1510), plus tiny vertical typewriter text at x ≈ 967.
- The animal is a ghostly grey see-through silhouette with a lighter inner structure. The parasite glows **orange/red** with bloom.
- Red target circles (double rings) and crosshairs. A slow push-in throughout.

## Titles
- A condensed heavy sans (Anton look) at the **top-left**. Left edge **x ≈ 99**, top **y ≈ 269** (just under the rule).
- Two lines: line 1 dark **#301c08** (cream **#d4ccbc** on X-ray), line 2 red **#bb4a32 to #c8442c** (it reads #bb573e after compression).
- Cap height varies with word length: a small line 1 is ~80–120 px; a big line 2 or a single word is ~135–190 px (font size ≈ cap/0.73). Leading is tight (gap 18–36 px). Max width ~800 px.
- Variants: a small typewriter kicker above the title ("AN", "NEXT", "NOT FICTION"); a single-line title; all-red numbers ("2 CM", "2X"); titles on **torn-paper strips** with a drop shadow ("THE BURROW", "DAY 1 → 3").
- **Pop-in:** line 1 appears on its word (fast fade or scale, ≈ 4 frames). Line 2 follows **0.2–0.6 s later**, on the next word. A struck word gets a red line drawn left to right, and the word fades to 60%.

## Props (all used in the reference)
Red name tag with a typewriter Latin name underneath; a cream label card with typing text; a hanging tag on a string that keeps swinging; a fabric/wood ruler that slides up with a red span highlight; a dimension bracket ("22 MM"); colour swatch cards that drop in rotated with captions; a checklist card (an X drawn in the box, red strike-through, a hand-drawn red ellipse); a status card with ticks and a red X strike; a terminal box ("> HACKING BRAIN_" typing, a progress bar filling to 100% that turns red, wires to a target); a red rubber stamp (slams in from about 1.6x scale with a blur, settles at a slight tilt); a film ticket torn in two; a magnifier (brass rim, wooden handle, 2x lens view); a stopwatch; a flip day counter ("DAY" plus two flipping digits) on a torn card; a big faded number behind the subject (salmon colour, ~25% opacity); a wooden specimen frame with labelled specimens popping in and a red circle; and a life cycle of oval cream cards joined by red dashed arrows that draw in.

## Motion vocabulary
Animals slide in from the frame edge (0.3–0.5 s, ease-out) then hold or crawl with a small bob. Labels slide or fade in. Text types letter by letter (~15–20 chars/s). Stamps slam in. Red lines draw on. Counters flip. Almost every action has a sound.

## Sound
- **Loudness:** -14.3 LUFS integrated, LRA 1.9 LU, so the voice is heavily compressed and always in front.
- **Bed:** a low, dark drone under the voice, quiet (gaps between words drop to about -40 to -48 dB). Music swells only in the last ~18 s.
- **SFX:** typewriter ticks, stamp thuds, paper slides, whooshes on cuts and similar small effects. They are short and kept well under the voice.

## Voice
- A Hindi male storyteller, energetic and mysterious. Hook: "सोचो..." followed by a reveal.
- Fast and nearly continuous: **2.6 words/s overall, 3.1 words/s in the hook**. Only 7 pauses over 0.25 s in the whole reel (longest 0.36 s). So the pauses at "..." are short beats, not long silences.

## Must not copy
No InShot watermark or other logos. Our watermark is **@speciman.files** in blackletter, bottom centre at about 78% height, cream with a soft shadow, about 70% opacity. Important text stays out of the bottom 20% and away from the right-edge button column.
