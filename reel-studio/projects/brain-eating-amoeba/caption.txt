Socho… paani ki ek boond mein aisa jeev, jo naak ke raaste seedha dimaag tak pahunch jaata hai 🧠💧

Naegleria fowleri ek single-cell amoeba hai jo garam meethe paani (talaab, jheel, nadi, garam jharne) mein rehta hai. Ye paani peene se nahi hota, aur insaan se insaan mein nahi phailta. Iska raasta bas ek hai: naak. Ye bimari bahut rare hai… darna nahi, bas saavdhaan rehna hai 🙏

✅ Talaab ya jheel mein koodo to naak band rakho ya nose clip lagao
✅ Garam paani ke jharnon (hot springs) mein sir paani ke upar rakho
✅ Naak mein paani daalna ho (wuzu ya jal neti) to ubaal kar thanda kiya hua ya distilled paani use karo
