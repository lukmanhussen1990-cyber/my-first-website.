# FILE No. 04: DENGUE: WHY THE SECOND TIME IS WORSE (production notes)

Same series look as FILE No. 01–03, in the normal style:
- paper and X-ray looks;
- Anton titles: the dark line, then the red line 0.45 s later;
- typewriter labels, stamps and checklists;
- the procedural sound kit and the dark drone;
- the **@speciman.files** watermark, which comes from the engine and so matches the snail reel exactly.

The file number is 04: the template's last reel was FILE No. 03. The Diwali reel is not in this repo.

Almost everything is drawn in code (`engine/js/infection.js`):
- **X-ray scenes:** the blood stream, the four dengue types (DENV-1 amber, 2 blue, 3 green, 4 violet), the Y-shaped antibodies, and the immune cell with its padlocked "door".
- **Mosquito life cycle:** the eggs and wriggling larvae.
- **Props and cards:** the 24-hour day clock, the house with the 100 m circle, the calendar, the medicine cards, the safety card and the specimen frame.

The GPT Image pictures are the mosquito, the cooler, the pot, the tyre and the bottle. The magnifier is reused from FILE No. 02.

## Rules followed
- No gore, no real patients: the body is shown only as cells, and the warning signs are text cards.
- No brand names and no doses: generic pills only, and "SEE A DOCTOR" is stamped on the medicine scene.
- No home remedies.
- Helpful rather than scary: the ending gives the three prevention steps, and the music warms from S17.
