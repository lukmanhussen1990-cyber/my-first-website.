⚠️ Bukhar utarne ke baad agle 24–48 ghante dhyaan rakho. Inmein se kuch bhi dikhe to turant hospital jao:
• Pet mein tez dard
• Baar-baar ulti
• Masoodon ya naak se khoon
• Bahut thakaan ya bechaini

✅ Subah-shaam poori baanh ke kapde + repellent
