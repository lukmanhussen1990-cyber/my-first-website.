# Setup — simple steps

This add-on has **two packs**. You need **both**.

- One pack makes things **work** (Behaviour).
- One pack makes things **look right** (Resources).

---

## Step 1 — Get the file

Download **`Luxury_Tech_Mycelium_X.mcaddon`** to your Android phone.

## Step 2 — Import it

1. Tap the file.
2. If Android asks which app, pick **Minecraft**.
3. Minecraft opens and says **Importing…**, then **Successfully imported**.

You do not need to unzip it.

**If tapping does nothing:** open the **Files** app, hold the file down, tap
**Open with**, then pick **Minecraft**.

**If the file ends in `.zip`:** rename it so it ends in `.mcaddon`.

## Step 3 — Make a world and turn on both packs

1. Tap **Play** → **Create New** → **Create New World**.
2. Tap **Behaviour Packs** on the left.
3. Tap **My Packs**. Find **Luxury Tech Mansion + Mycelium-X [Behaviour]**.
   Tap it → **Activate**.
4. Tap **Resource Packs** on the left.
5. Tap **My Packs**. Find **Luxury Tech Mansion + Mycelium-X [Resources]**.
   Tap it → **Activate**.

Turning on the first one usually turns on the second one for you.
**Check both lists anyway.**

## Step 4 — Turn on cheats

1. Tap **Game** on the left.
2. Turn **Activate Cheats** **ON**.

Without this, no commands work.

## Step 5 — Do you need experiments?

**No.** Leave all the experiment switches **off**. This add-on does not need them.

## Step 6 — Play

Tap **Create** and wait for the world to load.

Open chat (the speech bubble at the top) and type:

| Type this | What happens |
|---|---|
| `/function give_kit` | Gives you the mask, suit, scanner and medkits |
| `/function tech_house` | Builds the mansion where you stand |
| `/function outbreak_start` | Starts the outbreak |
| `/function myc_help` | Shows every command |

**Before you build the mansion:** stand somewhere **flat and open**.
It is a big building. It takes a few seconds to appear.

---

## How to use the scanner

Hold the **Mycelium-X Scanner**. Then either **tap** with it, or **sneak**.

It tells you how infected you are, and what is near you.

## If you get sick

- Green **HEALTHY** is fine.
- Yellow **EXPOSED** goes away on its own.
- Orange **INFECTED** gets worse by itself. Use a **medkit**.
- Red **CRITICAL** hurts you. Use a **medkit** now.

Wear the **Spore Mask** and the **Protective Suit** to catch less.

---

## Two things people get wrong

1. **Only one pack turned on.** You need both. If mobs look like white boxes or
   pink and black squares, the resource pack is off.
2. **Cheats are off.** If commands do nothing, go back to world settings and turn
   **Activate Cheats** on.
