My dog just became the mayor of my town. And honestly, he's doing better than the last guy.
The last mayor was so boring. His speeches were so long, people fell asleep standing up.
So before the vote, someone wrote my dog's name on the list.
Biscuit. As a joke. Then voting day came. Everyone laughed. And then everyone voted for him.
Biscuit got 4,000 votes. The old mayor got 12. On his first day, Biscuit sat on a stack of books
so he could reach his desk. And he started making new rules. Rule number one, nap time for everyone
at 2pm. The whole town. Even the bus driver. Rule number two, free belly rubs at the town hall.
The line went all the way around the block. Rule number three, every Friday is fetch day.
And the weird thing? Everyone was happier than ever.
Well, everyone except the old mayor. He stomped into the town meeting and shouted,
A dog can't run a town! Kick him out! Biscuit put his ears down.
I think he understood. But that same night, a little girl got lost in the forest.
It was dark. It was cold. And nobody could find her. Biscuit put his nose to the ground
and ran into the trees. We waited. One hour. Two hours. Then we heard a bark. It was Biscuit.
And the little girl was holding on to his collar. Her mom ran to her and hugged her so tight.
The old mayor didn't say anything. He just walked up to Biscuit,
knelt down, and gave him a belly rub. The next morning, Biscuit signed his
first law with a paw print. It said, Everyone gets a hug today. Even the old mayor.
