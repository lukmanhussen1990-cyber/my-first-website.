CREDITS - "Pooping on Top of Mount Everest" (37 s reel, @garden_reels)
=====================================================================

No attribution is legally required: every third-party asset in this video is CC0 / public domain, or SIL OFL (fonts).
Credits are listed as a courtesy and for provenance. No CC-BY material is used.

Voice
-----
Voiceover: supplied by the channel (ElevenLabs, mount_everest_poop.mp3), used as-is (only loudness-normalised).

Characters (MakeHuman / MPFB2)
------------------------------
MPFB2 Blender extension (MakeHuman Community) - tool.
MakeHuman system assets (CC0):
  - base mesh and "default" rig (163 bones incl. fingers and face), procedural high-poly eyes;
  - eyebrow001 / eyebrow006, eyelashes02 / eyelashes01, teeth_base, tongue01;
  - skin young_caucasian_male; hair short02 (friend).
Faceunits 01 - ARKit-style face shape keys by Mika Suominen (CC0).
Made for this video from the characters' own body meshes: quilted down suits with hood, mountaineering boots,
  mittens, black sunglasses, fur ruff strands.

Environment and props
---------------------
Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com):
  - HDRIs kloofendal_43d_clear_puresky (day) and syferfontein_6d_clear_puresky (golden hour);
  - textures snow_02, dark_rock_02, rock_06;
  - models rock_07, boulder_01, namaqualand_boulder_02, stone_01.
Modelled / procedural for this video:
  - summit terrain with sastrugi and boot prints, distant peaks, cloud sea, deep-blue sky;
  - prayer flags, dome tent, spindrift and blowing snow;
  - thermometer, ice blocks, camping stove + flame, hot rock, ice axe, backpack, carabiner;
  - toilet-paper roll and streamer, poop bag, summit flag;
  - steam, breath fog, ice-crystal burst, sparkles.

Fonts
-----
Poppins Bold / SemiBold - Indian Type Foundry, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (captions + watermark).

Music
-----
"Comedy Funny Background Song" by Almitory (OpenGameArt.org) - CC0 option of a multi-licensed entry.
  https://opengameart.org/content/comedy-funny-background-song
End sting built from CC0 jingles (see the sound list below).

Tools
-----
Blender 4.5 LTS (Cycles + OpenImageDenoise), MPFB2, faster-whisper (Whisper large-v3), Vosk/Kaldi aligner, ffmpeg.

SOUND EFFECTS / MUSIC CREDITS - "Pooping on Top of Mount Everest" SFX library
==============================================================================
All files in this folder are 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV. Every third-party source used is CC0 / public-domain dedication.
NO CC-BY material is used (the CC-BY grunt_effort_02..08 files of the older library were not touched).
Attribution is not required by any source; credits below are given as a courtesy and for provenance.

General processing applied to every output (on top of the per-file changes listed below): decoded and resampled to
48 kHz (ffmpeg), edited in numpy/scipy (cutting/trimming, layering, EQ/high-pass, varispeed, envelopes, short fades),
one-shots peak-normalised to -3 dBTP (true peak), loops made seamless by crossfading/circular construction,
written as 24-bit PCM. Downloads were treated as data only (nothing from a download was executed).
