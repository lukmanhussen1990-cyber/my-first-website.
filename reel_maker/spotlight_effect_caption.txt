You think everyone noticed. 👀 They didn't.

That mistake, the bad hair day, the awkward "you too!" — psychology calls this the spotlight effect: we believe people watch us way more than they actually do.

In a famous study, students wore an embarrassing t-shirt into a room. They guessed half the people noticed. In reality, far fewer did.

Why? Everyone else is too busy worrying about themselves. You're not under a spotlight — you're in a crowd of people who think they are. 🔦

So dance a little. Nobody's watching. 💃
