  brown dirt patches, BIG round gray boulders with dark speckle texture, flat polygonal stone slab "tables" on round boulders,

  shrubs with pink/red 5-petal flowers, low rolling green hills in distance, pale blue sky (lighter near horizon).

  leopard/cow-like blotches (rough suede texture).

  (blades blurred in foreground), wide shots, slow push-ins; shallow DOF (background soft).

  confetti at "Congratulations", gadget monitor sign "ANTI-STINK 9000" with sine-wave screen.

- Captions: Poppins-ExtraBold-like, white, thin dark outline + soft dark shadow, 1-3 words, centered ~58-60% down,
