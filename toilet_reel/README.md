# "You invented the toilet" — caveman reel, made 100% in code

A 9:16 Instagram reel (1080x1920, 30 fps) about an old caveman grandpa who invents the toilet,
in the style of the "caveman invents something" reference reels. Everything — character, world,
animation, effects, captions, sound effects and music — is generated by the code in this folder.
Only the voiceover is a recorded file.

## Pipeline

| Step | Script | What it does |
|------|--------|--------------|
| 1 | `src/body_def.py`, `src/sdf.py` | The grandpa sculpted as a signed-distance field (anatomical ellipsoids / round cones, eye sockets with eyelid skin, big nose & ears, skinny limbs, hands with knuckles), plus the one-shoulder torn leopard tunic and the neighbour's pointing hand. |
| 2 | `src/build_meshes.py` | Block-sparse marching cubes → meshes (head at 1.2 mm, body 1.6 mm) and per-part skin weights (compact smooth kernel). |
| 3 | `src/blender/build_character.py` | Blender: skeleton (spine, neck, head, jaw, eyes, eyelids, brows, cheeks, mouth corners, lips, arms, 5 fingers, legs, toes, beard/hair/skirt spring chains), skinning, eyeballs + eyelid shells, teeth/tongue, nails, procedural skin (rosy cheeks, wrinkles, age spots), leopard fur, rope belt, and ~20k hair/beard/mustache/eyebrow strands grown with gravity + collision. |
| 4 | `src/blender/build_scene.py` (+ `env_nature.py`, `props.py`, `fx.py`) | The garden world (grass clumps, speckled boulders, blobby trees, pink flower bushes, hills, sky, river, cliff + waterfall), all props (stone shovel, leaves, plank seat, clay bowl, yoke + pots, tank on poles with the FLUSH-O-MATIC 3000 sign, bamboo pipe), 3D cartoon effects and the light-blue explainer studio. |
| 5 | `src/blender/shots.py`, `anim.py`, `rig_core.py`, `poses.py` | 26 camera shots synced to the voice word timings: keyframed acting with easing/anticipation/overshoot, analytic two-bone IK with planted feet, procedural walks, breathing, blinks, facial expressions, hand shapes, spring follow-through for beard/hair/tunic, camera moves, shakes and depth of field. |
| 6 | `src/blender/render_shots.py` | Renders frames with Eevee (Blender 4.1) and records screen anchors for 2D overlays. |
| 7 | `src/audio.py` | Synthesises every sound effect and a bouncy background tune with numpy, ducks the music under the voice, mixes. |
| 8 | `src/overlay.py`, `src/assemble.py` | Word-by-word captions (Poppins ExtraBold, pop-in, face-avoiding), sparkles / pain star / wind streaks, watermark, and the final ffmpeg encode (H.264 + AAC, faststart). |

`data/words.json` holds the voiceover word timings (faster-whisper, corrected against the audio's silence gaps).

## Build

```bash
python3 src/build_meshes.py <mesh_dir> all
blender -b --python src/blender/build_character.py -- <mesh_dir> character.blend full
NEIGHBOUR_NPZ=<mesh_dir>/neighbour.npz blender -b character.blend --python src/blender/build_scene.py -- scene.blend
blender -b scene.blend --python src/blender/render_shots.py -- frames 1080 6 all
python3 src/audio.py voiceover.mp3 mix.wav 44.4   # then loudness-normalise to -14 LUFS
python3 src/assemble.py frames work mix_final.wav toilet_reel.mp4 1080 1920
```
