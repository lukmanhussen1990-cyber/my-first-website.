Why doesn't God just appear in the sky so everyone believes? 🌌

He already did that once. A whole nation saw fire on Mount Sinai and heard His voice. They stood afar off. Forty days later, they were dancing around a golden calf.

Seeing God didn't change their hearts.

God doesn't want to overwhelm you. He wants to be found by you.

"And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart." (Jeremiah 29:13, KJV)

"Blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed." (John 20:29, KJV)

He gives just enough light to follow, and enough room to choose.
