If God was going to flood the earth anyway… why make Noah build an ark for years? 🤔

God could have saved Noah in a second. Instead, Noah hammered for years in front of a laughing crowd — under a sky without a single cloud.

But the ark wasn't just a boat. It was a warning the whole world could see. Every nail, every beam, every year was another invitation.

“…the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing…” — 1 Peter 3:20 (KJV)

Noah was “a preacher of righteousness” (2 Peter 2:5). The ark had only one door (Genesis 6:16), and it stood open for years — until “the LORD shut him in” (Genesis 7:16).

Thousands of years later, Jesus said: “I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved…” — John 10:9 (KJV)

The door is still open today. 🚪✨
