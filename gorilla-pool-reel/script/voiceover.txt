1. If you hire an angry gorilla to dig your pool, it'll be done in seconds. Sort of.
2. But he digs so fast, the dirt lands on your neighbor. So you give him a wheelbarrow.
3. But the hole goes way too deep. So you show him a map with the perfect depth.
4. But the dirt walls cave in. So he pounds them flat, and you add concrete and tiles.
5. But now he's angry again. So you pay him in bananas, and he fills it with water.
6. Congratulations, you built a pool in record time.
