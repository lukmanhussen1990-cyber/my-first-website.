# Q.03 · SON OF THE FATHER: voiceover script

There are 18 parts. Read each part as written; the Bible quotes (parts 6, 7, 13 and 16) are KJV and must be word for word.
**Bold** words get a little extra weight. "Target" is about how long each part runs in the current edit. You don't
need to hit it exactly, because the whole reel re-times itself to your voice. Aim for about 65–75 seconds of speech in total.

## Pronunciation
- **Barabbas**: buh-RAB-us
- **Bar**: "bar"
- **Abba**: AH-buh
- **Aramaic**: air-uh-MAY-ik
- **twain**: "twayn"
- **Pilate**: PIE-lut

## Delivery
- Calm, warm and close to the mic, like telling one friend a secret. Don't preach or shout, even on "Barabbas" in P08;
  the crowd sound effects carry the shout.
- P09: leave a small breath after "son." before "Abba".
- P11 → P12 is one thought: the voice rises on "walked free…" and settles on "died in his place".
- P17: slow down on "you are Barabbas", then let "So am I." land softly.

## Recording
- Use a quiet room with no music, no effects and no noise filter. I add the music, sound effects and final loudness.
- **Best:** one file per part, named `P01` … `P18`, each with half a second of silence before and after. You can redo a
  single line without re-recording everything.
- **Also fine:** one file read straight through, with about one second of pause between parts.
- Any format works (WAV, M4A or MP3 from a phone). Send the file(s) in the chat.

When your audio arrives, I'll align every word, rebuild the timing (captions, cuts, sound hits, the name-card reveal)
to your voice, and re-render.
