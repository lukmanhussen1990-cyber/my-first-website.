In a moment, a crowd chose a murderer over Jesus. And that murderer's name will shock you. 👀

Every Passover, the governor set one prisoner free (John 18:39). Pilate brought out two men:
✝️ Jesus — who healed the sick and raised the dead.
⛓️ Barabbas — “a notable prisoner” (Matthew 27:16), a rebel “who had committed murder in the insurrection” (Mark 15:7).

“Whether of the twain will ye that I release unto you?” — Matthew 27:21 (KJV)
And the crowd shouted: “Barabbas.”

But notice his name. In Aramaic, “Bar” means son. “Abba” means father.
Barabbas means “son of the father.”

So that day, a guilty son of the father walked free… and the true Son of the Father died in his place.
“Then released he Barabbas unto them…” — Matthew 27:26 (KJV)

Barabbas didn't earn it. He didn't even ask for it. He walked out… because Jesus walked in.

“For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God…” — 1 Peter 3:18 (KJV)

And here's the truth: you are Barabbas. So am I. 🙏
