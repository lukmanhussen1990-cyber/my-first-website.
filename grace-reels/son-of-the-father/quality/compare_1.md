# Quality loop 1 — Q.03 SON OF THE FATHER

Three side-by-sides against the style reference (another creator's reels, studied locally, never committed):
`compare_1a.png` character close-up, `compare_1b.png` wide world shot, `compare_1c.png` verse card. Left =
reference, right = ours (render after the first round of shot fixes, `build/stills2`).

## 1a — character close-up (S1, Jesus, 4.0 s)
Where ours is weaker:
1. **Washed background.** The top half is a flat beige veil (sky glow + bloom + god rays + haze) and the crowd
   behind Him is reduced to faint blobs. The reference keeps strong, clean value shapes. → S1 grade: darker horizon
   and sun glow, bloom 0.62 → 0.5, rays 0.45 → 0.35, no haze, lighter DOF so the raised fists read.
2. **The bound hands read as a shrug.** The wrists end ~0.2 m apart with open palms out to the sides, so the rope
   seems to hang from one hand. → the bound pose is solved per view: the near arm reaches forward and the far arm
   angles back until both wrists meet in front of the belt (the rope sits on both).
3. **Sad instead of calm.** Eyes half closed and lowered, brows tilted: before He lifts His eyes He looks
   downcast. → gaze only a little lowered, neutral brows, eyes more open.
4. **Caption over a bright veil** has low contrast (white on beige). → fixed by 1 (the veil is gone); the caption
   band keeps its dark backing.
5. **Detail density.** The reference reads instantly because each figure is simple and stands on clear ground;
   ours stacks rope, sash, beard, label and bokeh in one area. → accepted for a hero close-up (the cinematic brief
   asks for it); the label stays on the chest, away from the face and the bound hands.

Where ours is ahead: real lighting (rim from the low sun, motion blur in the orbit, depth of field), facial
animation (eyes lift on cue), a living crowd behind Him, word-timed captions and a name label with its verse.

## 1b — wide world shot (S10, the swap, 52.0 s)
Where ours is weaker:
1. **Haze swallows the scene.** Storm fog and haze turn the palace and the city into grey blocks. → S10 fog
   0.006 → 0.0025 (+0.0015 as the storm builds), haze 0.02, contrast 1.14.
2. **Hard to find the subject.** Jesus led away is a small white figure among soldiers; the reference's subject
   reads at a glance. → less blur (DOF 0.6 → 0.45, wider focus range), clearer air (1); the camera ends higher.
3. **Foreground clutter.** Helmets and spears cut by the frame edge. → (2) and a cleaner path in front of the camera.
4. **Low value contrast.** Everything is mid-brown. → contrast up, split-tone kept.
5. **No label.** The reference labels its wides. → not added: the caption carries this line and the moment is
   emotional, not explanatory.

Where ours is ahead: depth (palace, city, palms, figures at three distances), motion (the crane, people walking,
slow motion), storm light building toward the hill.

## 1c — verse card (S13, 1 Peter 3:18, 68.3 s)
Where ours is weaker:
1. **The light burst sits behind the card.** The break in the clouds is centred behind it, so the cream card has
   no edge against a cream glow. → the light now breaks just above the hill and the cross (below the card).
2. **Unspoken words too faint.** The karaoke ghost (72 % paper) makes the end of the verse almost invisible;
   the reference shows the whole verse at once. → ghost words at 58 % paper (gold 52 %), still clearly "not yet".
3. **Background competes.** Strong rays all over the frame. → (1); rays now radiate from the hill.
4. **Keyword weight.** JUST / UNJUST in gold with an underline are lighter than the reference's maroon caps. →
   kept gold (the reel's palette), the darker ghost (2) raises the overall ink contrast.
5. **Card shape.** The reference is a clean rounded card with an accent bar; ours is deckled parchment with rules.
   → kept (the series' own style), it reads clearly once (1) gives it an edge.

Where ours is ahead: the card floats and tilts in 3D light, words warm from ghost to ink as they are spoken, gold
words pop on their word, the underline draws, and the card sits over the hill where the light breaks through.
