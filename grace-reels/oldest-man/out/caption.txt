The oldest man who ever lived died the same year as the flood. Coincidence? 👀

If you add up the ages in Genesis 5:
Methuselah was 187 when Lamech was born.
Lamech was 182 when Noah was born.
Noah was 600 when the flood came.
187 + 182 + 600 = 969 — exactly the age Methuselah reached. He died the year the flood came. ⏳

“And all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty and nine years: and he died.” — Genesis 5:27 (KJV)

Some say his name can mean “when he dies, it shall be sent.” (The meaning is debated.)

“…the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing…” — 1 Peter 3:20

God waited as long as He could. And He is still waiting today — not to punish you, but for you. 🙏
“The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.” — 2 Peter 3:9
