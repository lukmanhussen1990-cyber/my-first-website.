# Quality loop 3a — verse card (Genesis 5:24, S3, frame 469 / 15.63 s)

`compare_3a.png`: left = reference card frame (refB_055, Deuteronomy 30:19 card, 720×1280 upscaled to 1080×1920),
right = ours (S3 Genesis 5:24 card, all words spoken, gold run underlined). The 3D behind is still placeholder boxes;
judged on the overlay only.

## Where ours is weaker
1. **Verse type too small, card reads as a flat banner.** Ours: 54 px italic over a 792 px measure, so the verse
   falls into 2 long lines in a wide, short strip. Ref: ~63 px (scaled) in a compact 3-line block that reads at a glance
   and looks like a card. → 62 px, ~740 px measure (3 lines), taller card.
2. **Gold keywords lack contrast.** `GOD TOOK HIM.` in orange-gold `#a5640c` on cream is about 3.3:1, and the thick
   orange underline competes with the letters. Ref's maroon caps are about 8:1. → deep antique-gold ink, a hair of
   dark-brown edge, slightly larger caps, and a thinner, lighter brush underline under the run.
3. **No quotation signal.** The ref wraps the verse in “ ” with hanging punctuation, so it reads as a quote at once.
   Ours has none. → hanging “ ” around the verse and a large, faint gold opening-quote ornament in the corner.
4. **Paper looks grey and dirty, not premium.** Mottling and speckles are too strong, the double rule is busy, and the
   deckled corners look ragged on square corners. The ref card is clean, warm and crisp. → cleaner warm cream, texture
   at about half strength with fewer specks, 16 px radius with only a whisper of deckle, one gold-foil hairline frame
   and small corner flourishes, a warm top-light gradient.
5. **Placement and backdrop.** Our card sits high (top 292 px), crowding the header, and the darkening is heavy
   (up to 62 % black at the edges), which kills the golden-morning scene. The ref keeps the scene bright and the card in
   the upper-middle. → card top ~360 px, lighter vignette (about 18–34 %) centred on the card. Also the reference line
   `GENESIS 5:24 · KJV` is pale gold on cream (low contrast) → darker gold-brown.

## Where ours is already ahead
Real paper (fibres, soft long shadow plus contact shadow, slight 3D tilt and float, glint on landing), word-by-word
karaoke (ghost → ink, current-word marker), gold words pop on their spoken word, and the underline draws as they are spoken.
