## What the reference does well (copy the STYLE only)
**World**
- A warm low-poly diorama: faceted trees, rocks, hills and sheep, seen from a high three-quarter "miniature" angle (about 50–60°).
- Soft long shadows, light haze, and a soft blur at the top and bottom of the frame (tilt-shift feel).
- Each scene is graded to ONE color mood: olive for the garden, orange for the desert, grey for sorrow, dark brown for the call to action. Big mood changes happen at scene cuts.

**People**
- Flat 2D paper-doll figures with a dark brown outline (about 3 px at 720 wide, so about 5 px at 1080) and matte flat colors.
- Simple robes with a belt stripe. A mix of skin tones.
- Figures stand on the 3D ground with a soft contact shadow.
- Faces are very minimal (a dot eye). **We do better:** expressive eyes with a highlight, brows, mouths, and real acting.

**Light and effects**
- God-ray light beams with floating sparkles, and a glowing pool of light on the ground.
- Fog and haze transitions, and objects dissolving into dust.
- A glowing path along the ground (meaning: "the way").

**Text**
- Captions sit top-left (about 6% side margin, about 23% from the top) in an italic serif (Cormorant-like), cream-white, at most 2–3 lines.
- Words appear one by one; the next word shows as a faint ghost.
- Keywords are bold upright caps in an accent color. A soft darker blurred band sits behind the caption block.
- Header: small mark + series name top-left, episode name top-right, tiny letter-spaced sans caps.
- Labels: small dark rounded tag, white letter-spaced sans caps, with a thin leader line to the object.
- Scripture: cream paper card with rounded corners and a soft drop shadow, italic serif with bold caps keywords, and the reference in small letter-spaced caps under it ("BOOK 0:0 · VERSION").
- Call to action: dark warm background, simple icons, a cream pill comment box that "types", and small floating hearts.
- End: the logo mark draws itself on a dark background.

**Pacing**
- The caption changes every 2–4 s and the scene changes every 4–8 s.
- The camera never fully stops (slow drift or push).
- The hook question is shown in the first second.

## NEVER copy from the reference
- Their channel name, logo mark (red gate-like symbol), series title, episode title or "The Tree" header.
- Their coral-red keyword color and dark-red card accents.
- Their characters (Adam, Eve, the snake, the walking man), their script lines, or their "gift or burden" call to action.

## Improvements over the reference for The Corners
1. Expressive faces + a 2D bone rig (blinks, breathing, seeded springs on scarves, robes and beards).
2. 2.5D parallax: sky / far / mid / near planes with a long-lens camera, plus foreground wheat.
3. The gold family-line tracker under the captions keeps people watching.
4. Light has meaning: glowing corners = God's provision, and the gold vine = His plan.
5. Sound design hits on dot pops, grain landings and the "?" → JESUS reveal.
