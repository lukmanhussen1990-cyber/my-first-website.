# Style notes: reference reel study

Source: `reference/reference_01.mp4`, 720×1280, 30 fps, 89.4 s, AAC 44.1 kHz.
Method: 1 frame per second into `reference/frames/` (89 frames), all frames viewed, close-up crops at
full size, colour sampling, scene-cut detection, loudness scan, and a word-timed transcript.
Pixel sizes are scaled ×1.5 to our 1080×1920 frame.

> **Copy the style only.** Never copy their brand ("HONEST QUESTIONS"), their red logo, their
> series title ("THE TREE"), their red keyword colour, their characters (Adam, Eve, the snake) or
> their script lines.

---

## 1. Characters

| Item | What the reference does |
|---|---|
| Build | Flat 2D paper-cut people, almost always in **pure side profile** facing left or right. |
| Head-to-body | About **1 : 5.5** (measured 1:5 to 1:6). Heads are a little big, bodies tall and thin. |
| Eye | **One almond eye**: white with a round dark pupil, near the front of the face. Blinks are drawn as a short closed line. |
| Nose | A **small rounded bump** on the profile line. |
| Mouth | **No mouth.** Emotion comes from poses (hand on head, kneeling, reaching). |
| Hair / beard | One flat dark shape each (`#21140C` to `#3C2A12`). Beards cover the lower face. |
| Outline | **Dark brown, not black** (about `#281E0C`). Even thickness, about 1/80 of figure height (4 px on a 300 px figure). Rounded joins. |
| Shading | Flat fills. The **far arm and far leg are one shade darker**. No gradients, no texture. |
| Skin | Warm browns of many tones, from light tan to deep brown. |
| Clothes | Simple A-line tunics or robes. Muted flat colours: sand `#C9A46E`, sage band `#7A9A55`, teal `#4E8C7A`, red `#C0504D`, mustard `#D9A93B`, purple `#7A5BA8`, off-white `#EDE7DD`. Belts are flat bands of another colour. |
| Hands and feet | Arms are one long rounded tube. **Hands are rounded mitten ends with no fingers.** Legs are straight tubes ending in rounded "L" feet. |
| Poses | Standing, walking (scissor legs, small arm swing), reaching or offering, pointing, kneeling, lying down, hand to forehead in grief. Clear silhouettes. |
| Motion | Simple puppet rig: limbs rotate at the shoulder and hip, walks have little body bob, blinks happen. Characters fade in and out like ghosts when the story jumps. |
| Shadows | A soft, blurred, dark contact blob under the feet. No hard shadow shapes on the body. |

## 2. World

- **Low-poly 3D diorama.** Trees are faceted spheres on brown cylinder trunks. There are faceted palms
  with spiky leaves, low-poly sheep, plain boxes for houses and tablets, rocks as faceted lumps, and the
  river as a flat dusty-blue ribbon (`#5E8A9A`) with thin pale dashes.
- **Camera:** a high three-quarter angle (about 35–45° down) with a long lens that feels almost
  orthographic. It moves all the time, slowly: push-ins, pans and drifts. It sometimes goes close for
  character moments (0–2 s, 67–69 s).
- **Light:** a warm sun from the **upper left**. **Long, soft, semi-transparent shadows fall to the
  lower right**, in a darker olive or brown of the ground colour, never black.
- **Holy light:** a soft vertical **beam from above**, warm white-gold, with a glowing oval pool on the
  ground, **floating gold dust specks** and bloom. It is used for "God's view", the cross and the
  choice moments.
- **Haze:** a strong warm atmospheric haze. The top of the frame melts into a soft band of the sky
  colour. Foreground objects such as palm leaves are **blurred (depth of field)**.
- **Grade per scene** (dominant colours sampled):
  - Garden olive: `#828741 #939749 #9F9253`, shadow `#5A5C2F`
  - Desert orange: `#D08F50 #B1733B #C98343`, dark `#764D2A`
  - Market beige: `#D0A767`
  - Grey "no choice" void: `#6A6A62`
  - Maroon "sickness": `#70453E #523122`
  - Call-to-action dark brown: `#2E1F16 #1D130D`
- **Contrast** is low and colours are a little desaturated, with a soft vignette. Highlights are never
  pure white except inside light beams.
- **Small FX:** dust-ring puffs when things vanish, ghost cross-fades of people, and a glowing path strip.

## 4. Pacing, transitions and sound

- **Voice starts on frame 0** with no intro. It ends at 86.4 s, followed by about 3 s of logo outro.
- Narration is **fast: about 187 words per minute** overall, about 218 wpm while speaking. Pauses
  between phrases are 0.4–0.7 s (mean 0.51 s).
  **Ours will be calmer: about 145–155 wpm**, with the planned pauses.
- A caption block holds one sentence or clause (about 2–6 s). A new block replaces the old one.
- Scenes change every **4–10 s** and the camera never stops moving.
- There are almost **no hard cuts**: one at 49.0 s (grey void cuts to golden light, for contrast) and
  one into the outro at 87.4 s. Every other change is a **soft dissolve through haze or blur**, a ghost
  cross-fade of characters, or a colour-temperature shift.
- **Loudness:** −14.1 LUFS integrated, true peak −0.8 dBFS, loudness range 3.6 LU. There is a
  **continuous music bed** with no silence until the outro.

## 5. Our target: the same family, but better

1. Full **1080×1920** (theirs is 720×1280), with crisp outlines.
2. Real **2.5D parallax** (sky / far / mid / near planes), haze between layers, bloom, and a per-scene grade.
3. Richer acting: breathing, blinks, weight shifts, follow-through on hair, beards and cloaks, and
   anticipation before big moves.
4. More readable text: bigger captions (66 px vs about 54 px) with a proper scrim, safe zones respected.
5. Our own brand: gold and cream colours, our own logo, header and end card.

## 8. Lessons from "They Never Died" (GPT Image test 1)

What worked:
- GPT Image gave **real PNG transparency** on every image, with clean edges.
- One **turnaround + parts + poses** set per character. The parts sheets became bone rigs (Elijah,
  Enoch) that walked and talked well. The pose drawings carried the big moments (kneel, reach, rise).
- Pasting the same **character bible** text into every prompt, and attaching the first approved sheet
  as a style anchor, kept faces and colours consistent.

What to fix this time:
- Only 7 of 35 images got made, so the list must be **short and clearly ranked**. Group props on one
  sheet, characters first.
- Pose sheets came out at a different scale from the turnarounds. Ask for **one fixed figure height**.
- No backgrounds came in, so the worlds were built in code (clean but plain). For Friend, ask for
  **layered backgrounds** with the scene light already painted in.
- Characters and props should be drawn in **neutral, even light**, so the same art works by
  candlelight, torchlight and daylight. Scene light, rim light and flicker are added in code.

## 9. Night and interior light for "Friend" (our extension of the style)

The reference has no night scenes. We keep the same flat paper-cut people and low-poly world, and add
cinematic light:
- **Torch and candle light:** warm orange to gold (#FF9A4A to #FFD08A), soft falloff, a gentle flicker,
  embers.
- **Moonlight:** a cool fill and rim (#9FB4D8). Shadows are deep blue-violet (#1E2236, #2B2440), never
  black.
- **Upper room:** low oil lamps on the table, warm amber walls (#7A4E2E, glowing to #C9894E), dark corners.
- **Olive garden:** a big moon in a deep blue sky (#1B2440 to #32456B), olive trees as dark low-poly
  shapes with silver-green leaves where the moon touches them, and a line of torches in warm orange.
- **Feast (daytime):** the reference's bright, warm palette.
- **Jesus** is always the calmest and brightest point in the frame.
