# Style notes — reference reels

Written in Phase 1 of *The Sunset Rule* (idea 49). Update it when new reference reels are added.

**Sources** (local only, not in git): `reference/ref_1.mp4` (99.5 s, "why did people in the Bible live so long") and `reference/ref_2.mp4` (89.5 s, "why didn't God remove the tree"). Both are 720×1280, 30 fps, AAC 44.1 kHz. 1 fps frames are in `reference/frames/` (188 frames). Measurements below are given at 720p and converted to our 1080p canvas (×1.5).

Style refs for ChatGPT, cropped so they show no text: `episodes/sunset-rule/style_refs/`
- `style_ref_1.png`: close-up. Two robed figures and a low-poly tree (ref_2 @ 67 s).
- `style_ref_2.png`: wide scene. Market stalls with awnings in sandy haze (ref_2 @ 26 s).
- `style_ref_3.png`: group shot. A family of four beside a wooden frame (ref_1 @ 28 s).

## 1. Characters
- **Build:** flat 2D paper-cut figures about 4.5–5 heads tall (the man is about 1 : 4.4, the woman about 1 : 5). The head is one rounded shape and the neck is hidden.
- **View:** almost always side or 3/4 side view, facing the action. Front views are rare.
- **Face:** one almond-shaped white eye with a dark round pupil and a dark outline, and a small pointed nose bump. There is no mouth, no eyebrows and no visible ear. A beard is one dark shape that covers the jaw. Their figures barely have expressions, so we will need simple eyebrows for anger and grief.
- **Outline:** very dark brown (≈ #201008), the same thickness everywhere, about 3 px at 720p (≈ 4.5 px at 1080p). Inner overlaps such as an arm over the body or a sleeve edge are outlined too.
- **Colors:** flat fills with almost no shading on the people. Skin is warm brown (≈ #AA6A3A) to dark brown, and hair is dark brown. Robes are tan (≈ #A88046), white, blue-grey or olive. The sash is one band of contrast color (olive ≈ #687038, or red).
- **Hands and feet:** arms are long thin tubes with a rounded end. There are no fingers (a round mitten end). Tunics have cap sleeves. Legs are two straight tubes, and feet are a rounded L-shape with no toes.
- **Poses:** simple standing, a scissor-leg walk, one arm reaching, carrying something. There is very little acting; figures mostly slide or walk across the set.
- **Modern people:** the same build in tank tops and jeans, in bright solid colors (mustard, red, white, purple).
- Their Jesus-like figure (ref_1 @ 85 s) has a white robe, a red sash and long brown hair, and walks in a light beam. **We do not copy this design.** Ours has an off-white robe and a blue-grey sash.

## 2. World
- **A real 3D low-poly diorama.** It has faceted icosphere tree canopies, palms, box buildings, slab props (tables, the ark frame), low-poly rocks and a ribbon river. The flat characters stand in the 3D set as billboards.
- **Camera:** a high 3/4 "diorama" angle (about 35–50° down) through a long lens that looks almost orthographic. Moves are slow drifts and push-ins, with now and then a big pull-back from a close-up to a wide (ref_2 0→3 s).
- **Depth:** strong tilt-shift depth of field, with the foreground and background blurred and the middle sharp. Dark, blurred foreground rocks and leaves sit in the corners and act as a natural vignette.
- **Palette:** each scene is a near-monochrome wash, tinted by thick haze that matches the sky.
  - olive green for the garden
  - sand and orange for the desert and market
  - warm brown for dark interiors
  - cool grey for sin, war and "no choice"
  - dusty red for violence and sickness
  - deep brown-black (≈ #261A12) for icon and chart scenes
- **Light:** soft and hazy, with long soft ground shadows (trees cast big blurry shadows). Their signature effects are:
  - a vertical god-ray beam with a glowing pool of light on the ground
  - floating dust motes
  - light-leak flash transitions
  - a glowing light path along the ground
- **Density:** low. A few big shapes per scene, lots of empty ground, sparse dressing (a few trees, rocks, sheep).
- **Icon scenes:** flat glowing icons on a dark warm-brown backdrop, such as an hourglass, an open book with rays, a candle, a cracked heart, a gift box, bar charts and countdown numbers.

## 3. Text and graphics
- **Header:** always on screen. A small logo and brand name sit top-left, and the series title sits top-right, in white letter-spaced sans caps. The text is about 13 px at 720p (≈ 20 px at 1080p), at y ≈ 70 (≈ 105).
- **Captions:**
  - white italic serif (Cormorant style), about 44 px at 720p (≈ 66 px at 1080p)
  - left-aligned at x ≈ 44 (≈ 66); the first line sits at y ≈ 300 (≈ 450), sometimes at y ≈ 100
  - 1–3 lines, then the text clears and the next phrase starts
- **Word reveal:** words appear in sync with the voice, and the next word shows as a faint ghost just before it is spoken. Keywords switch to UPPERCASE in coral red (≈ #E2654C), in the same font but slightly bolder.
- **Labels:** a small dark pill (≈ #1B1512, about 90%) with white letter-spaced caps (about 10 px at 720p). It sits centered under the subject or has a thin leader line to it. The format is "NAME · BOOK 5:27".
- **Verse cards:**
  - cream paper (≈ #EAE2D3), about 90% of the frame width, rounded about 10 px, with a dark-red accent bar on the left edge (≈ #8A3428)
  - the quote is centered in italic serif (≈ 45 px at 1080p), with keywords in dark-red bold caps
  - the reference sits below in small letter-spaced caps ("1 PETER 3:20 · KJV")
  - the card fades in as a ghost and holds about 4–6 s
- **Big numbers:** gold serif numerals with a small letter-spaced caps label below.
- **Ending:**
  - a question to the viewer, then "Tell us in the comments."
  - a cream comment pill that types an answer, plus two choice chips (green and red)
  - then 2–3 s of dark screen while the logo draws itself

## 4. Pacing and transitions
- **The voice starts on frame 0** with the hook question, which runs 0–5 s. There is no title card, no intro and no fade from black.
- **Narration:**

  | | words | pace |
  |---|---|---|
  | ref_1 | 252 | ≈ 157 wpm |
  | ref_2 | 269 | ≈ 187 wpm |

  Sentence gaps are 0.3–0.8 s, and the longest pause is about 1.5 s. The last word lands about 3 s before the end, which leaves room for the logo.
- **Shots:** a new visual idea every 3–6 s, but very few hard cuts (ref_1 has about 12, ref_2 has 1). They mostly use cross-dissolves, light flashes, color shifts inside the same set (garden → grey → red) and camera moves through one set.
- **Structure:** hook question → story and facts → verse or twist → reflection → personal question → call to comment.

## 5. Sound
- **Loudness:** about −14.1 LUFS integrated, with an LRA of about 3 LU, so the mix is heavily compressed.
- **Music:** a warm, bass-heavy bed. Most of its energy is at 60–250 Hz, with almost nothing above 2 kHz. It stays loud in the gaps (≈ −20 dB RMS against ≈ −16 dB for speech) and swells on the end card.
- **Effects:** very few audible ones.

## 6. Copy, never copy, beat it
**COPY (style only):**
- flat paper-cut people with dark outlines and almond eyes
- a warm low-poly diorama world with haze, depth of field and dust
- italic-serif word-by-word captions
- pill labels and cream verse cards
- a dark-screen ending with a logo that draws itself

**NEVER COPY:**
- their brand name and logo (a red gate-like mark and "HONEST QUESTIONS")
- their series titles ("LONG LIVES", "THE TREE")
- their coral-red keywords and red card accents. Ours are bright gold #F2B544 on dark and deep gold #9A5B0C on light, and red #C0392B only appears in the anger glow.
- their character designs
- their script lines and questions

**HOW WE BEAT IT:**
1. **Sharper.** Native 1080×1920, crisp outlines of about 5 px, and higher-resolution art (AI-upscaled where needed).
2. **Cinematic light.** One global `sunTime` drives the sky, light color, shadow length and direction, and the color grade in every scene, plus god rays, bloom and real long shadows.
3. **Real acting.** Rigged characters with expressions (brows, eyes, a tear), the 12 principles, secondary motion (robes, hair, doves, coins, dust) and speed ramps. Theirs mostly slide.
4. **Closer cameras for emotion.** Face close-ups and a hand macro, not only tiny wide shots.
5. **Sound.** Frame-accurate effects, music that moves from D minor to D major, and a voice-forward mix (music ducks about 8 dB) at −14 LUFS.
6. **Captions.** Gold keywords on a soft scrim so they are always readable, plus the sun-line countdown motif.

## 7. How this becomes the GPT Image STYLE BLOCK
These choices come from the notes above. The full block is in `episodes/sunset-rule/GPT_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.
- **Lines and shapes:** a dark brown outline (#2A1A10) of even thickness, simple shapes, and matte flat color with one soft shadow tone.
- **People:** about 4.5 heads tall, with tube limbs, rounded mitten hands with a small thumb, simple rounded feet, and an almond eye, small nose and no mouth. We add simple short eyebrows so the acting can show anger and grief.
- **Backgrounds:** soft, even light with no visible sun, because the sun direction, sky and shadows are added in code from `sunTime`.
