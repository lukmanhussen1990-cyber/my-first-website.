# Wheat and Weeds: script and shot list

Idea 68, 100% code episode (no GPT Image, no downloaded pictures). Matthew 13:24–30, Romans 3:23,
2 Peter 3:9, Acts 9.

## Script (*word* = keyword in gold)

1. If God is real… why doesn't He just stop all *evil*? Right now?
2. Jesus told a story about a *field*.
3. A farmer planted good *seed*.
4. "But while men slept, his *enemy* came and sowed tares among the wheat, and went his way."
5. When the weeds came up, the servants asked: "Sir, didst not thou sow good seed in thy field?"
6. And he said: "An *enemy* hath done this."
7. God didn't plant the evil.
8. So the servants asked: should we pull up the weeds?
9. But notice his answer: "*Nay*; lest while ye gather up the tares, ye root up also the *wheat* with them."
10. "Let both grow together until the *harvest*."
11. Here's the hard truth: if God pulled out all evil tonight… who would be left?
12. "For all have *sinned*, and come short of the glory of God."
13. He's not slow. He's *patient*: "not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance."
14. Saul once hunted Christians. Then he met Jesus… and became *Paul*.
15. If God had pulled him up early, we'd never have his letters.
16. One day, the *harvest* will come. If God ended all evil tonight, would you be ready? Yes, or not yet?

The flow was only polished by splitting long lines into separate voice clips (1, 11, 13, 14 and 16);
every fact and quote is unchanged.

## Care rules

- God the Father is never shown as a person, only as light. Jesus is a simple, dignified figure with no halo.
- The enemy is only a faceless hooded shadow: no horns, no monster face, not scary for children.
- No burning at the harvest. Family-safe, kind, non-denominational; the tone is hope and patience.
