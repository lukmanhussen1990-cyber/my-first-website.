Why doesn't God just stop all evil right now?

Jesus answered with a story about wheat and weeds (Matthew 13:24–30).
Pull the weeds too early and you tear up the wheat with them.
God isn't slow. He's patient, "not willing that any should perish" (2 Peter 3:9).

"This is a faithful saying, and worthy of all acceptation, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners; of whom I am chief." (1 Timothy 1:15)
