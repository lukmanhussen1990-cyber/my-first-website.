## A) Script

No changes. The flow works, and every quote matches the KJV list word for word (Mark 3:5, John 2:16, 1 Peter 2:23, Luke 23:34, Ephesians 4:26). `*word*` = gold keyword on screen.

1. Jesus got *angry*. So… is anger a *sin*?
2. In a synagogue, a man stood there with a withered hand. And some people watched Jesus, hoping to catch Him breaking the rules.
3. The Bible says Jesus "looked round about on them with *anger*…"
4. But read the rest of that verse: "…being *grieved* for the hardness of their hearts."
5. He was angry and heartbroken… at the same *time*.
6. Then He healed the man *anyway*.
7. Another time, He made a whip of cords and *flipped* the tables in the temple.
8. "Make not my Father's house an house of *merchandise*."
9. But notice who He got angry *for*: God's house, and hurting people. Never for *Himself*.
10. When they mocked Him and hurt Him, "when he was reviled, reviled not *again*."
11. Instead He said: "Father, *forgive* them; for they know not what they do."
12. Angry about what hurt others. *Forgiving* about what hurt Him.
13. And the Bible gives anger a *deadline*: "Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the *sun* go down upon your wrath."
14. Did you notice? The sun has been *setting* this whole time.
15. Is there someone you need to forgive before it goes down? Just type *TODAY*.

## A2) Voiceover, in 8 parts

Paste each part into your voice tool as one take. This is plain text with no asterisks; the keywords only turn gold in the captions. I cut each part into sentences and set the pauses myself: 0.3 s between sentences, 0.6 s after "at the same time", 0.8 s before "But notice" and "Did you notice?", and 0.6 s before the last line.

**PART 1 — Hook** (line 1) — *punchy and curious*

```
Jesus got angry. So… is anger a sin?
```

**PART 2 — The synagogue** (lines 2–6) — *storyteller; a little slower on the quotes*

```
In a synagogue, a man stood there with a withered hand. And some people watched Jesus, hoping to catch Him breaking the rules. The Bible says Jesus "looked round about on them with anger…" But read the rest of that verse: "…being grieved for the hardness of their hearts." He was angry and heartbroken… at the same time. Then He healed the man anyway.
```

**PART 3 — The temple** (lines 7–8) — *stronger, more energy*

```
Another time, He made a whip of cords and flipped the tables in the temple. "Make not my Father's house an house of merchandise."
```

**PART 4 — But notice** (line 9) — *firm and clear*

```
But notice who He got angry for: God's house, and hurting people. Never for Himself.
```

**PART 5 — When He was hurt** (lines 10–12) — *soft and tender*

```
When they mocked Him and hurt Him, "when he was reviled, reviled not again." Instead He said: "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do." Angry about what hurt others. Forgiving about what hurt Him.
```

**PART 6 — The deadline** (line 13) — *slow and serious*

```
And the Bible gives anger a deadline: "Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath."
```

**PART 7 — The reveal** (line 14) — *quiet wonder*

```
Did you notice? The sun has been setting this whole time.
```

**PART 8 — Call to action** (line 15) — *warm, gentle invitation*

```
Is there someone you need to forgive before it goes down? Just type today.
```

**Recording tips**

- Use the same voice and the same settings for all 8 parts: a calm, warm male storyteller.
- Keep normal speed. All 8 parts together should come to about **72–78 s**.
- Voice only: no music, no effects, no reverb. MP3 or WAV.
- Name the files `part1` … `part8`. Re-record only the part that came out wrong.
- Read "an house" exactly as written. It is the KJV wording.

## C) Images for ChatGPT

**How to make them**

1. Make **#1 `char_jesus_turnaround.png` first**. Every other Jesus image attaches it, so Jesus looks the same everywhere.
2. Keep #1–#7 in **one** ChatGPT chat (the "Jesus chat"). Use a new chat for each other group (people, animals, props, backgrounds, skies).
3. For each image: attach the files listed under **Attach**, then paste the whole prompt. Each prompt already starts with the STYLE BLOCK and ends with the size.
4. Make **#25 `sky_noon.png`** before the other skies. #26–#30 are recolors of it, so all six skies line up and blend smoothly.
5. **Download the original PNG.** Don't take screenshots: they lose the transparency and the sharpness. On a phone a transparent PNG can look white or black; that's normal.
6. Redo an image if it has any text or labels, extra fingers, parts cut off at the edge, or a Jesus who looks different from #1.
