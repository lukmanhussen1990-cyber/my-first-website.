# Friend: script and shot list

Idea 44 · one-time GPT Image episode (your GPT Image art; voice, music, sound, captions and animation in code).
Target length 70–85 s. **Planned length about 73 s.**

## A) Script (final)

`*word*` = keyword (gold caps in the captions). The flow already reads well aloud, so **no wording was
changed**. Every fact and quote is exactly as approved.

1. Judas betrayed Jesus with a *kiss*. Jesus answered with one *word*.
2. But first, go back a few hours.
3. That night, Jesus took a towel, knelt down, and washed His disciples' *feet*.
4. All twelve. Including *Judas*.
5. And Jesus already knew: "For he knew who should *betray* him."
6. Judas had sold Him for "thirty pieces of *silver*."
7. But notice: in the Law, thirty shekels of silver was the price of a *slave*.
8. That's what Jesus was worth to him.
9. Later that night, in a garden, Judas came with a crowd, torches and swords.
10. He walked up… "Hail, master"… and *kissed* him.
11. And Jesus said: "*Friend*, wherefore art thou come?"
12. *Friend*. To the man betraying Him.
13. That's who Jesus is. He washes the feet of people who hurt Him.
14. "When we were *enemies*, we were reconciled to God by the death of his Son."
15. They called Him "a friend of publicans and *sinners*." They were *right*.
16. Could you wash the feet of someone who betrayed you? Yes or no?

Voice pauses (from the brief): 0.3 s between sentences, 0.6 s after "Including Judas", 0.8 s before
"But notice", 1.0 s of silence before "Friend, wherefore art thou come?", and 0.7 s after "To the man
betraying Him". The music turns from D minor to D major on "That's who Jesus is".

### Care rules

- Jesus has no halo and no realistic face (one almond eye and a small nose, like everyone in this style).
  He is always the calmest, brightest point in the frame.
- The crowd and the men who pay Judas are generic and unnamed: no uniforms, symbols, badges or
  features of any group. Nobody is blamed but Judas's own choice.
- Family-safe: swords and staves are held up, never used. The kiss is a respectful greeting on the cheek.
