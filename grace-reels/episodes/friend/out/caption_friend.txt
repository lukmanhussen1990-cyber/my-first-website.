Judas betrayed Jesus with a kiss. Jesus answered with one word: "Friend."

Hours earlier, Jesus knelt and washed Judas's feet. He already knew (John 13:11).
He still called him friend as the betrayal happened (Matthew 26:50).
That's who Jesus is. He loved us while we were His enemies (Romans 5:10).

"So they weighed for my price thirty pieces of silver." (Zechariah 11:12)
