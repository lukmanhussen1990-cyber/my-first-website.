## Special rules

- God is shown only as light (the beam that takes Enoch, the golden opening in the storm, the light on
  the mountain). No figure stands for God.
- Jesus is a simple shining figure in white: no halo, and a plain face with no realistic features.
- Everything is family-safe and respectful: no violence; the chariot's fire harms no one.
