# They Never Died: voiceover parts (you record, I edit)

**15 parts = 15 audio files.** Paste only the text inside each box into your voice tool (no asterisks,
no notes). Use the **same voice and the same settings** for every part.

## Settings

- **Voice:** a calm, warm male storyteller (your usual reel voice).
- **Pace:** calm, about **145–155 words per minute**. That is slower than the reference reel (about
  187 wpm). All 15 parts together should come to about **65–72 seconds**.
- **File type:** WAV (48 kHz or 44.1 kHz) is best. MP3 (192 kbps or more) or M4A is fine.
- **Dry voice only:** no music, no echo or reverb, no sound effects, no background noise.
- **Leave about 0.3 s of silence** at the start and end of each file. Don't cut tight.
- **No clipping:** keep the loudest peaks around −3 dB.
- **Names:** `vo_01.wav` … `vo_15.wav` (or `.mp3` / `.m4a`).
- **If you use ElevenLabs:** model *Multilingual v2*, Stability about 50%, Similarity about 75%,
  Style about 15%, Speaker boost on, Speed about 0.95. If one word sounds wrong, regenerate only
  that part.

I build every pause myself: 0.3 s between parts, 0.7 s before part 12, and 0.8 s before "But read it
again". So don't worry about the gaps.

## The 15 parts

### vo_01: hook · about 4 s · stress **died**

```text
Two men in the Bible never died. Not even once.
```

Calm but intriguing, like sharing a secret. Take a short beat after "died", then say "Not even once"
softer and slower.

### vo_02: Enoch · about 6.5 s · stress **Enoch**, **took**

```text
The first was Enoch. "Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him."
```

Say "The first was Enoch" simply. Then read the verse slowly and reverently, with tiny pauses at the
colon and the semicolon and gentle weight on "took".

### vo_03: translated · about 6.5 s · stress **translated**

```text
"By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death..." because he pleased God.
```

Slow scripture voice with a lift on "translated". Pause at the "...", then warmly: "because he pleased God."

### vo_04: Elijah · about 4 s · stress **Elijah**, **wrong**

```text
The second was Elijah. And most people get his story wrong.
```

Turn the page: "The second was Elijah." Then lean in, a little playful: "And most people get his story wrong."

### vo_05: walking · about 3 s

```text
Elijah and Elisha were walking and talking...
```

Relaxed storytelling, slightly slower. Let "talking..." trail off: something is about to happen.

### vo_06: chariot · about 4.5 s · stress **fire**

```text
When suddenly, "there appeared a chariot of fire, and horses of fire."
```

Rising energy. Say "When suddenly" a little faster, then fill "a chariot of fire, and horses of fire"
with wonder.

### vo_07: the myth · about 5.5 s

```text
Most people think Elijah rode that chariot to heaven. But read it again.
```

Say the first sentence matter-of-factly. Then **pause about 1 second**, and say "But read it again"
slower and quieter, like a secret.

### vo_08: between · about 4 s · stress **asunder**, **between**

```text
The chariot "parted them both asunder." It came between them.
```

Clear and deliberate. Put weight on "asunder", then "It came between them" with weight on "between".

### vo_09: whirlwind · about 3.5 s · stress **whirlwind**

```text
And "Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven."
```

Awe. Slow. Lean on "whirlwind", and let "heaven" ring out softly.

### vo_10: my father · about 5 s

```text
Elisha cried, "My father, my father"... and he saw him no more.
```

Tender and emotional: say "My father, my father" with feeling, not shouting. Pause at the "...", then
"and he saw him no more" quietly.

### vo_11: the cloak · about 2.5 s

```text
Only his cloak came floating down.
```

Very soft and slow, almost a whisper at the end.

### vo_12: the mountain · about 6.5 s · stress **Jesus**

```text
But that's not the end. Centuries later, on a mountain, Elijah appeared again... talking with Jesus.
```

Say "But that's not the end" with a warm smile and a hint of excitement. Then tell the rest as a story,
pause at the "...", and make "talking with Jesus" bright and warm.

### vo_13: the promise · about 2.5 s · stress **you**

```text
And Jesus made a promise for you:
```

Personal, warm and slower, with weight on "you". End open, because the promise comes next.

### vo_14: never die · about 4 s · stress **never**

```text
"Whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die."
```

The heart of the reel. Slow, warm and confident. Don't rush "liveth" and "believeth". Put strong but
gentle weight on "never".

### vo_15: your question · about 5 s · stress **question**

```text
If you could ask Elijah one question, what would it be? Comment below.
```

Friendly and inviting, like talking to a friend. Lean a little on "question". Keep "Comment below" light
and short.
