## A) Script (final)

`*word*` = keyword (gold caps in the captions). The flow already reads well aloud, so **no wording was
changed**. Every fact and quote is exactly as approved.

1. Two men in the Bible never *died*. Not even once.
2. The first was *Enoch*. "Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God *took* him."
3. "By faith Enoch was *translated* that he should not see death…" because he pleased God.
4. The second was *Elijah*. And most people get his story *wrong*.
5. Elijah and Elisha were walking and talking…
6. When suddenly, "there appeared a chariot of *fire*, and horses of fire."
7. Most people think Elijah rode that chariot to heaven. But read it again.
8. The chariot "parted them both *asunder*." It came *between* them.
9. And "Elijah went up by a *whirlwind* into heaven."
10. Elisha cried, "My father, my father"… and he saw him no more.
11. Only his cloak came floating down.
12. But that's not the end. Centuries later, on a mountain, Elijah appeared again… talking with *Jesus*.
13. And Jesus made a promise for *you*:
14. "Whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall *never* die."
15. If you could ask Elijah one *question*, what would it be? Comment below.
