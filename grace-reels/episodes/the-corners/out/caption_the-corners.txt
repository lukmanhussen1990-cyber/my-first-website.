If God is real… why are there poor people? 🌾

God answered in a farming rule: leave the corners of your field for the poor and the foreigner (Leviticus 19:9–10).
A poor widow named Ruth picked up leftovers in a field like that. She became the great-grandmother of King David, and from David’s family came Jesus.
God’s answer to poverty was always people with open hands. People like you.

“Truly I tell you, whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of Mine, you did for Me.” — Matthew 25:40 (BSB)
