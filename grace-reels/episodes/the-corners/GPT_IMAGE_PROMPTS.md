## 1. Script (words kept exactly as your voice file says them; *word* = gold keyword)

1. If God is real… why are there *poor* people?
2. God answered that question… in a *farming* rule.
3. He told farmers: "When you reap the harvest of your land, you are not to reap to the very *edges* of your field."
4. Leave the corners. "Leave them for the *poor* and the foreigner."
5. So poor people could always find food. It was built right into the law.
6. And God said: "Open wide your *hand*."
7. Now watch what happened to one poor widow named *Ruth*.
8. Her husband had died. She had no money. And she was a *foreigner*.
9. She said: "Please let me go into the fields and *glean* heads of grain."
10. Glean means she picked up the *leftovers*… at the corner of a field.
11. But notice. She just happened to pick a field that belonged to a kind man named *Boaz*.
12. Boaz married her. And Ruth became the great-grandmother of King *David*.
13. And from David's family came… *Jesus*.
14. Jesus came from a woman who picked up *leftovers* in a field.
15. "Though He was rich, yet for your sakes He became *poor*."
16. And Jesus said: "Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of Mine, you did for *Me*."
17. God's answer to poverty was always people with *open* hands. People like you.
18. What's one way you can help someone this week? One word.

**Quote check (BSB):** Lev 19:9 ✓ · Lev 19:10 ✓ · Deut 15:11 ✓ · Ruth 2:2 ✓ · 2 Cor 8:9 ✓ · Matt 25:40 ✓.
**One small note:** Ruth 2:3 says Ruth gleaned "behind the harvesters". Line 10 ("at the corner of a field") is a fair simplification of Lev 19:9, so I kept it, since it is already in your recording.

## 3. How to make the images in ChatGPT

1. Use **one ChatGPT chat** for everything, and make the images **in this order** (characters first).
2. Attach the files listed under "Attach" for each prompt. Style refs are in `episodes/the-corners/style_refs/`. After you make an image, attach that file to the later prompts that list it.
3. Ask for one image per message. If a figure looks different from earlier images, say: *"Same character as the attached image, same face and colors."*
4. Download every image as **PNG** and name it exactly as shown.
5. Put them all in **`grace-reels/episodes/the-corners/assets/gpt/`**, then type **DONE**.

**Sizes:** P = 1024×1536 (portrait) · L = 1536×1024 (landscape)
**MUST** = needed · **NICE** = nice to have (I have a code fallback)
