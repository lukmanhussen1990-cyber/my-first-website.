## Script (as recorded; *word* = keyword in gold)

1. Moses asked to see God's *face*.
2. And God said: if you do… you will *die*.
3. Moses had already seen fire, plagues, and a sea split in two.
4. But on the mountain, he asked for more: "I beseech thee, shew me thy *glory*."
5. God answered: "There shall no man see me, and *live*."
6. So God hid him "in a clift of the *rock*"… and covered him while His glory passed by.
7. Moses only saw His *back*.
8. But notice what happened next. When Moses came down, his face was *shining*.
9. And he didn't even know it. "Moses wist not that the skin of his face *shone*."
10. The people were *afraid* to come near him.
11. That was just from being *near* God.
12. Now here's the best part. Centuries later, Jesus said: "He that hath seen *me* hath seen the *Father*."
13. Moses had to hide in a rock to see God's back.
14. You can see God's glory "in the face of Jesus *Christ*."
15. If you could see God's face, what would you say first? One line.

## Two small changes to the shot list (please check)

1. **S11 is set in the upper room, not on a Galilee hillside.** Jesus said John 14:9 to Philip at the Last
   Supper in Jerusalem (John 13:1–2; the talk only moves on at John 14:31, "Arise, let us go hence"). It
   also saves images: the upper room, the table and the disciples from Friend are reused as they are.
   The green Galilee hillside moves to S12–S13, where Jesus welcomes people and children.
2. **The montage follows the voice:** fire, then plagues, then the sea ("fire, plagues, and a sea split in
   two"). The FIRE badge fills on "fire" and the SEA badge on "sea".

## Care rules

- God is never shown as a person, a face or a hand. His glory is only light, made in code. The covering
  in the cleft is a soft shadow of light and dark, never a hand shape.
- Jesus is a simple, dignified figure with no halo.
- Family-safe, respectful, kind and non-denominational.
