## Care rules

- God is never shown as a person, a face or a hand: His glory is only light made in code (the opening in
  the storm, the river of light, the trails of light). The covering in the cleft is a soft shade with no
  shape.
- Jesus is a simple figure with no halo; the low sun is kept off to the side, never behind His head.
