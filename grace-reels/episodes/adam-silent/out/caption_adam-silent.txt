Adam was right there. Why didn't he stop her? 🍃

Genesis 3:6 says Eve gave the fruit to her husband "with her." He was there the whole time, and he said nothing.

Then everyone passed the blame. Adam blamed Eve, and even God. Eve blamed the snake. We still do it today.

But in another garden, Jesus didn't hide. He stepped forward and took the blame for us.

"For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin." (2 Corinthians 5:21)

Who do you blame? Be honest 👇
