Voice

Character (MakeHuman / MPFB2)
-----------------------------
MPFB2 Blender extension v2.0.18 (MakeHuman Community) - tool.
MakeHuman system assets (CC0): base mesh, "default" rig (163 bones incl. fingers + face), procedural eyes,
  eyebrow001, eyelashes02, teeth_base, tongue01, skin young_caucasian_male, clothes male_casualsuit03
  (re-textured as orange spotted fur - no logo t-shirt).
Faceunits 01 - ARKit-style face shape keys by Mika Suominen (CC0).
Hair strands, third arm, heart eyes, sparkles: generated/modelled for this video.

Environment
-----------
Poly Haven (CC0, https://polyhaven.com): HDRI kloofendal_43d_clear_puresky; textures concrete_layers_02,
  thatch_roof_angled, white_rough_plaster, rough_wood, weathered_brown_planks, dark_wooden_planks,
  fine_grained_wood, forrest_ground_01, brown_mud_leaves_01.
Modelled/procedural for this video: cooling tower + toy tower (hyperboloid lathe, V-leg ring), volumetric steam
  and dust, cottage (walls, thatch roof, timber frame, arched door, windows with crack/glow, stone chimney blocks,
  interior), wooden mailbox "theoddtilt", stone phone, yellow cable + plug, broccoli trees, spotty boulders,
  grass and flowers.
